Sarot bronchus clamp

A clamping forceps with curved, grooved jaws (left- and right-sided versions to match the bronchus) and ring handles, designed to occlude the bronchus without crushing its wall.

How it is used

Used in lobectomies and pneumonectomies to clamp the main bronchus before it is divided and closed (sutured or stapled).
