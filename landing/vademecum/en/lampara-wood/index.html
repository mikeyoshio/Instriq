Wood's lamp

Long-wave ultraviolet lamp (~365nm) that provokes visible fluorescence in certain pigments and fungal/bacterial metabolites when shone on the skin.

How it is used

Non-invasive diagnostic examination in the dermatology clinic for scalp tineas, pityriasis versicolor, erythrasma, pigmentary disorders (vitiligo), and porphyrias.

Clinical tip

It must always be interpreted together with the clinical picture, to avoid false positives/negatives.
