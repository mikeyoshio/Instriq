Piezoelectric oral surgery motor

Ultrasonic micro-vibration generator (25-30 kHz) that cuts mineralized bone tissue without damaging adjacent soft tissue.

How it is used

Precision osteotomies in oral surgery and implantology: sinus lifts, third-molar extraction, bone splitting.
