Optical urethrotome (Sachse urethrotome)

A rigid sheath with a built-in optical telescope and a retractable cold knife that can be advanced under direct vision.

How it is used

Incises a urethral stricture under direct vision (direct-vision internal urethrotomy, DVIU), classically with a single cut at the 12 o'clock position through the scar tissue.

Clinical tip

Recurrence is common, especially with long or already-recurrent strictures; urethroplasty is generally preferred over repeat urethrotomy in that situation.
