Laser safety goggles

Eyewear with optical filter lenses (glass or polycarbonate with absorptive or reflective coatings) designed to block or attenuate specific laser wavelengths, labeled with the optical density (OD) and wavelength range for which they provide protection.

How it is used

Mandatory for all operating-room personnel whenever a surgical laser is active (e.g., holmium, thulium, CO2 laryngeal, dermatologic, ophthalmic YAG, or retinal argon lasers), as it protects the retina and cornea from a reflected or direct laser beam that could cause irreversible eye injury.

Clinical tip

Laser safety goggles are NOT universal: each pair is rated for a specific wavelength range and optical density (EN 207 standard in Europe, ANSI Z136.1 in the US), so staff must always confirm the goggles match the exact laser in use before activation; the wrong goggles can look protective while offering none.
