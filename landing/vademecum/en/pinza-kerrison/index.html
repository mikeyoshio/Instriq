Kerrison rongeur

A punch-type rongeur with a fixed distal footplate and a sliding blade that bites through bone as the handle is squeezed; available in multiple widths and angulations (straight, up-biting, down-biting).

How it is used

Used to remove vertebral lamina and bone edges during laminectomy, laminotomy and foraminotomy, and to enlarge craniectomy margins; one of the most frequently used instruments in spinal stenosis and disc herniation surgery.

Clinical tip

Advance the blade under control and without tenting the underlying dura, since durotomy is a well-described complication of this instrument.
