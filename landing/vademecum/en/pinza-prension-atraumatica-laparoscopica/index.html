Atraumatic laparoscopic grasping forceps

Fenestrated grasper with long curved jaws and fine serrations distributing pressure evenly.

How it is used

Used as the non-dominant-hand instrument for atraumatic traction/exposure of bowel, stomach or gallbladder during dissection.
