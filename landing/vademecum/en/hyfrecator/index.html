Hyfrecator (electrodesiccator)

A low-power monopolar electrosurgery device generating high-frequency current delivered through a fine needle-tip or ball electrode.

How it is used

Used for electrodesiccation and electrocoagulation of superficial skin lesions (angiomas, warts, keratoses) and for spot hemostasis during biopsies.

Clinical tip

Unlike a surgical electrosurgical unit, it operates at very low power settings, making it suitable for minor office-based procedures under local anesthesia.
