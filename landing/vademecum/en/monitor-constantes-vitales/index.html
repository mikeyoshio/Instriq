Multiparameter vital signs monitor

A device that continuously displays, on a screen with configurable alarms, several of the patient's physiological parameters (ECG, heart rate, oxygen saturation, non-invasive blood pressure, respiratory rate and, often, temperature) from sensors and electrodes attached to the patient.

How it is used

Used as standard in the operating room, the ICU and the recovery room for continuous patient surveillance during surgery, anesthesia and the immediate postoperative period.

Clinical tip

It can be expanded with optional modules (invasive pressure, capnography, cardiac output) plugged into the same rack.
