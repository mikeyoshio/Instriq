Suction tips: how to tell them apart

Look at the tip and whether it has a hole to control suction with a finger.

Yankauer cannula

Rigid and curved, with a bulb tip: open field and mouth.

Frazier suction tip

Fine and angled, with a thumb hole to control suction: neurosurgery and ENT.

Poole cannula

Multi-perforated sleeve: suctioning large volumes in the abdomen without catching tissue.
