Dissecting forceps: how to tell them apart

First the tip (teeth or not), then the size.

Toothed dissecting forceps

Teeth at the tip: hold skin and firm tissue without slipping.

Non-toothed dissecting forceps

Cross serrations and no teeth: delicate tissue.

Adson forceps, toothed

Short, broad grip and a narrow toothed tip: skin.

Adson forceps, toothless

Like the toothed Adson, but with a serrated tip: fine tissue.

DeBakey atraumatic vascular forceps

Long, with fine atraumatic longitudinal serrations: vessels and bowel.
