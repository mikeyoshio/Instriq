Haemostatic forceps: how to tell them apart

Sort them by size and check the tip: teeth (Kocher), right angle (Mixter) or serrations on half the jaw (Kelly).

Mosquito forceps (Halsted)

The smallest, with fine tips and fully serrated jaws: small vessels.

Kelly forceps

Medium size; the classic Kelly is serrated only on the distal half of the jaw.

Rochester-Pean forceps

Large and strong, fully serrated jaw without teeth: pedicles and thick tissue.

Kocher forceps

Like a Pean, but with 1x2 teeth at the tip: fibrous tissue, not delicate vessels.

Mixter right-angle forceps

Right-angled tip: passing ties behind vessels and ducts.
