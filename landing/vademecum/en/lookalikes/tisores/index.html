Dissecting scissors: how to tell them apart

Look at the handle-to-blade ratio and how thick the blades are.

Mayo scissors

Short and sturdy with heavy blades: dense tissue and cutting sutures.

Metzenbaum scissors

Long handles and short, fine blades: delicate tissue, never suture.

Iris scissors

Small with very fine tips: fine tissue, ophthalmic and plastic surgery.
