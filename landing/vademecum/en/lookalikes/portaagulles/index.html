Needle holders: how to tell them apart

Rings or not, and size. And don’t mistake it for a haemostat: a needle holder has a much shorter, wider jaw.

Mayo-Hegar needle holder

Finger rings and ratchet, short cross-hatched jaw: the general needle holder.

Mathieu needle holder

No finger rings: curved spring handles with the lock at the end.

Castroviejo needle holder (microsurgery)

Small, spring-loaded with flat handles: microsurgery and ophthalmology.
