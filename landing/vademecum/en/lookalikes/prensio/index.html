Tissue graspers: how to tell them apart

Teeth at the tip mean the tissue may be damaged; a rounded window means atraumatic.

Allis forceps

Tip with small interlocking teeth: firm grip, for tissue that will be removed.

Babcock forceps

Rounded, fenestrated tip without teeth: atraumatic, for bowel and tubes.

Duval lung forceps

Wide, triangular, fenestrated tip: holding lung tissue in thoracic surgery.

Ring forceps (Foerster)

Oval rings at the tip: holding a swab or grasping the cervix.
