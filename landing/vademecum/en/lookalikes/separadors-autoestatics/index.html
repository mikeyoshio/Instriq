Self-retaining retractors: how to tell them apart

Rakes (Weitlaner), a single point (Gelpi), an abdominal frame (Balfour) or a thoracic crank (Finochietto).

Weitlaner retractor

Two arms with several rake-like prongs and a ratchet.

Gelpi retractor

A single sharp point on each arm: small, deep incisions.

Balfour retractor

A frame with two side blades and a central one: holds the laparotomy open.

Finochietto rib retractor

Crank-driven rack with blades: spreads the ribs or the sternum.
