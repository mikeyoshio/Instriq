Hand-held retractors: how to tell them apart

Check the size and curve of the blade: the deeper the layer, the longer and more curved.

Farabeuf retractor

C-shaped, double-ended plate: superficial layers.

Langenbeck retractor

A single right-angled blade on a long handle: superficial and middle layers.

Richardson retractor

Large right-angled blade with a broad handle: abdominal wall.

Deaver retractor

Long blade curved like a question mark: deep abdomen.

Malleable retractor

A flat strip that is bent by hand into whatever shape is needed.
