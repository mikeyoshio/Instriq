Instruments that get mixed up

Families of instruments that look alike, with the feature that tells each one apart.

  • Dissecting scissors — Mayo scissors, Metzenbaum scissors, Iris scissors
  • Dissecting forceps — Toothed dissecting forceps, Non-toothed dissecting forceps, Adson forceps, toothed, Adson forceps, toothless, DeBakey atraumatic vascular forceps
  • Haemostatic forceps — Mosquito forceps (Halstead), Kelly forceps, Rochester-Pean forceps, Kocher forceps, Mixter right-angle forceps
  • Tissue graspers — Allis forceps, Babcock forceps, Duval lung forceps, Ring forceps (Foerster)
  • Needle holders — Mayo-Hegar needle holder, Mathieu needle holder, Castroviejo needle holder (microsurgery)
  • Hand-held retractors — Farabeuf retractor, Langenbeck retractor, Richardson retractor, Deaver retractor, Malleable retractor
  • Self-retaining retractors — Weitlaner retractor, Gelpi retractor, Balfour retractor, Finochietto rib retractor
  • Suction tips — Yankauer cannula, Frazier suction tip, Poole cannula
