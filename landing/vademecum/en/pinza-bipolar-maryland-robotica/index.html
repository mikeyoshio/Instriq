Robotic Maryland Bipolar Forceps

Fine, non-fenestrated bipolar forceps for precise dissection and hemostasis of small vessels.

How it is used

Use: fine hilar/vascular dissection, hemostatic control of small-caliber vessels in urology, general surgery, gynecology.

Clinical tip

Confirmed as a distinct named product on both the da Vinci and Hugo RAS platforms, unlike the fenestrated bipolar forceps (broader, for atraumatic grasping).
