Lone Star retractor

A retractor system consisting of a lightweight ring and multiple elastic stay hooks that are individually placed around an incision or natural orifice.

How it is used

Used mainly in pediatric anorectal surgery (for example, endorectal pull-through for Hirschsprung disease or anorectal malformations) to retract the anus and expose the rectum without continuous manual assistance.
