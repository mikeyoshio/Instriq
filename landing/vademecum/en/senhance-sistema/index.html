Senhance Surgical System

Asensus Surgical's digital laparoscopy robotic platform, with independent cart-mounted arms, an open surgeon console, eye-tracking camera control and haptic feedback.

How it is used

Used in minimally invasive general, gynecologic, urologic and thoracic surgery (colectomy, hysterectomy, adrenalectomy), leveraging haptic feedback to modulate force applied to tissue.
