Straight (Keith) needle

A straight needle, typically with a triangular cutting point, designed to be held and passed directly with the fingers or forceps, without a needle holder.

How it is used

Hand-held skin closure, retention sutures, and passing suture in confined procedures where a needle holder is impractical, such as penile prosthesis placement.

Clinical tip

Unlike curved needles, which require a needle holder, straight needles are handled directly, which makes them unique for certain specific technical steps.
