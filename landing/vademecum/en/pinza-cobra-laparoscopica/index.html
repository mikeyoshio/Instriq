Cobra laparoscopic grasper

Double-action laparoscopic grasper with curved, serrated tips, offering more grip than a smooth atraumatic grasper, available in 3-5 mm.

How it is used

Used to securely grasp small portions of tissue where a smooth atraumatic grasper would slip, such as biopsies or friable fragments.
