Mechanical thrombectomy system

Console for continuous or catheter-based aspiration, combined with mechanical thrombus-extraction devices.

How it is used

Percutaneous extraction of venous or arterial thrombi (pulmonary thromboembolism, acute arterial ischemia) without thrombolysis.
