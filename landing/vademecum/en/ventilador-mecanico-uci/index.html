Mechanical ventilator (ICU)

A device that generates and controls gas flow to the patient's lungs according to programmed parameters (volume or pressure, rate, PEEP, FiO2), replacing or assisting spontaneous breathing.

How it is used

Used in patients with respiratory failure who require invasive ventilatory support (connected to an endotracheal or tracheostomy tube) in the ICU, in the operating room during general anesthesia, or during transport (transport version).

Clinical tip

Smaller, reduced-volume variants exist for neonatal/pediatric ventilation, with flow sensors adapted to very small tidal volumes.
