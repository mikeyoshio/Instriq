Wound protector-retractor

Two rigid rings joined by a cylindrical polymer sleeve, placed in the extraction incision.

How it is used

Protects wound edges and keeps them retracted during specimen extraction in laparoscopic resections, reducing wound infection; combined with a gel cap, enables hand-assisted laparoscopic surgery.
