Percutaneous renal access (Chiba-type) needle

Fine (18-22G), flexible needle with a short (~30°) bevel, used under fluoroscopic, ultrasound or endoscopic guidance to puncture the target renal calyx.

How it is used

The first step of percutaneous nephrolithotomy (PCNL): establishes the renal tract that is then dilated over a guidewire and fitted with the Amplatz sheath already catalogued.
