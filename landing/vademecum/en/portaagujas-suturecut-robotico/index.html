Robotic SutureCut Needle Driver

Needle driver with an integrated cutting blade in the jaw to cut suture without an instrument exchange.

How it is used

Use: running sutures and anastomoses (vesicourethral, pyeloureteral, bowel) with console-controlled suture cutting.
