Cheek retractor for buccal mucosa graft harvesting

Retractor that exposes the cheek's buccal mucosa to facilitate harvesting it as a graft.

How it is used

Used while harvesting the buccal mucosa graft for substitution urethroplasty, the reference technique for complex urethral strictures.
