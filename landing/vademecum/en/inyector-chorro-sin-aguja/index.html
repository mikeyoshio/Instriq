Needle-free jet injector

Spring- or compressed-gas-powered injector that fires a metered dose (typically 0.1-0.2 mL) through a micrometric orifice as a high-pressure jet, penetrating intact epidermis without a needle.

How it is used

Used to anesthetize the donor and recipient areas in hair transplantation, and in dermatology for intradermal corticosteroids and minor cutaneous procedures, avoiding the pain and anxiety of needle puncture in patients who require dozens of injections.

Clinical tip

It only infiltrates the first few millimeters of dermis, so it is typically used as superficial anesthesia prior to deeper infiltration with a conventional needle; the anesthetic reservoir must be emptied and autoclaved between patients to avoid cross-contamination.
