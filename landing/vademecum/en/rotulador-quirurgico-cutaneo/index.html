Surgical skin marking pen

Sterile single-use pen with biocompatible ink (typically gentian violet) for marking the clinical margin of a lesion before excision.

How it is used

Preoperative marking of the safety margin (e.g. 4-5mm in non-melanoma skin cancer) after examining the lesion under good light and, if needed, a dermatoscope.

Clinical tip

Published cases of iatrogenic tattooing from retained ink recommend disclosing the risk in the consent form and cleaning the mark off at the end.
