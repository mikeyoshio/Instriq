da Vinci SP Single-Port Robotic System

Single-access robotic platform with a flexible endoscope and three double-jointed arms through one 2.5 cm incision.

How it is used

Use: prostatectomy, cystectomy, transvesical/retroperitoneal partial nephrectomy, minimal-access transoral/breast surgery.

Clinical tip

Mechanically and clinically distinct from the generic multi-port da Vinci system: both the access and the instrument set differ.
