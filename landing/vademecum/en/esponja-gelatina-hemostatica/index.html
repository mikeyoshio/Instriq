Absorbable gelatin hemostatic sponge (Gelfoam)

A porous, pliable, sterile sponge made of purified porcine gelatin, off-white or tan in color, supplied dry as compressed sheets, blocks, or a powder that can be cut or shaped to size.

How it is used

Placed or packed onto a bleeding surface (e.g., raw bone, liver, or soft tissue) as a topical absorbable hemostat; on contact with blood or fluid it swells and forms a mechanical scaffold that supports clot formation, and is fully resorbed by the body over several weeks.

Clinical tip

Pre-moisten it with sterile saline (dry, it does not conform well and can act as a wick), and avoid over-packing a closed space, since it expands considerably once it absorbs fluid and can compress nearby structures (e.g., a nerve or the spinal canal).
