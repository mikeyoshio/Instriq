Cavernotome

Long instrument with a retractable cutting blade at the tip, in several lengths.

How it is used

Creating a channel through fibrosed corpora cavernosa before penile prosthesis placement.

Clinical tip

Always advance it laterally, away from the urethra and septum, to avoid perforation.
