Cushing forceps

Dissecting forceps with tips offset in a "bayonet" shape relative to the handle axis, toothed or toothless.

How it is used

Dissecting and grasping tissue at the base of a craniotomy or narrow neurosurgical corridor, where a conventional forceps' straight handle would block the surgeon's line of sight.

Clinical tip

The bayonet shape is shared by neurosurgical scissors and bipolar forceps for the same reason: keeping the hand out of the microscope's line of sight.
