Lucas curette

A double-ended curette with an oval, sharp-edged scoop at each tip, mounted on a straight handle.

How it is used

Used in oral surgery to curette the dental alveolus after extraction, removing granulation tissue and cystic remnants.

Clinical tip

Overly aggressive curettage can delay alveolar healing, so its use should be limited to visible pathological tissue.
