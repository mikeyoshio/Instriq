Osteotome

A wedge-shaped instrument with a cutting edge beveled on both sides, designed to cut, divide or shape bone.

How it is used

Struck with a surgical mallet, it is used to cut or reshape bone during osteotomies, joint replacement surgery and fracture surgery.

Clinical tip

Unlike a single-beveled bone chisel, an osteotome is beveled on both sides, which helps it cut in a straighter line.
