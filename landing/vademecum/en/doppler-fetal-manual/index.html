Handheld fetal Doppler

A handheld portable device with a low-frequency Doppler ultrasound probe, distinct from the already-catalogued intraoperative vascular Doppler, with a speaker or display showing the heart rate.

How it is used

Intermittently auscultates fetal heart rate at prenatal visits or during low-risk labour, as a simpler alternative to continuous CTG monitoring.
