Low-intensity shockwave therapy device

Low-intensity acoustic shockwave generator, applied externally to the penis via a handheld probe.

How it is used

Stimulates angiogenesis and improves vascular flow in vasculogenic erectile dysfunction; a non-surgical outpatient treatment.

Clinical tip

Unlike PDE5 inhibitors, its effect builds up over a course of several sessions rather than being immediate.
