Andrology microsurgery operating microscope

Floor-standing optical microscope with motorized zoom providing up to 15-25x magnification and coaxial illumination of the surgical field.

How it is used

Essential for spotting sperm-containing seminiferous tubules in micro-TESE and for performing the millimeter-scale anastomosis in vasovasostomy, vasoepididymostomy, and varicocelectomy.

Clinical tip

In micro-TESE, tubules most likely to contain sperm appear larger, more opaque, and whiter under the microscope.
