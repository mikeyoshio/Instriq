Metzenbaum scissors

Scissors with fine, delicate blades, longer and narrower than the Mayo. Available in short and long lengths.

How it is used

Fine dissection of delicate tissue (fascia, vessels, subcutaneous tissue).

Clinical tip

Never used to cut sutures: the fine blades dull and lose precision.
