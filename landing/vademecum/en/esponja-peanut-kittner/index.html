Peanut sponge (Kittner dissector)

A small, firm, sterile, oval, peanut-shaped roll of gauze, sometimes with a cotton core, mounted in the tip of a clamp (such as a sponge-holding forceps or tonsil clamp) rather than held by hand.

How it is used

Used for blunt dissection, to gently separate tissue planes, clear away loose connective tissue, or blot small amounts of blood in a confined surgical field, taking advantage of its firm, rounded shape and the control afforded by the clamp.

Clinical tip

Always keep it mounted on the clamp and never allow it to be used loose in the wound; like any other small gauze item, it must be included in the formal count to prevent it from being inadvertently left behind.
