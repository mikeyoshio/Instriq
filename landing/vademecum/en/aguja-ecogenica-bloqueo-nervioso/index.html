Echogenic peripheral nerve block needle

An insulated, short-bevel needle with a tip finish that enhances its ultrasound reflectivity, distinct from the spinal/Tuohy needles already catalogued.

How it is used

Puncture for single-shot peripheral nerve blocks, guided by ultrasound and/or nerve stimulation, complementing the peripheral nerve stimulator already in the catalogue.
