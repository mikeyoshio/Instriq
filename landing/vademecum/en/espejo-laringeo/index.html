Laryngeal mirror

A small round mirror with an adjustable angle, mounted on a long thin handle, lightly warmed before use to prevent fogging.

How it is used

Used for indirect laryngoscopy, allowing visualization of the tongue base, epiglottis, and vocal cords in classic office-based examination.

Clinical tip

Although fiberoptic systems have largely replaced it, it remains useful as a quick, inexpensive screening tool.
