Peritoneal dialysis (Tenckhoff) catheter

Silicone catheter with side holes at the abdominal end and one or two Dacron cuffs that sit in the wall.

How it is used

Access for peritoneal dialysis in children and adults with kidney failure.

Clinical tip

Before closing, check fluid flows in and out freely; the tunnel and cuff are placed as the surgeon directs.
