Knight septal scissors

A long, angled scissors with blades curved in-plane to facilitate cutting within the narrow nasal cavity.

How it is used

Used in septoplasty to cut deviated septal cartilage and bony spurs, facilitating their resection or reshaping.

Clinical tip

The blade angulation allows cutting without obstructing the operator's view, unlike a conventional straight scissors.
