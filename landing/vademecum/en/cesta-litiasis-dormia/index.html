Stone retrieval basket (Dormia basket)

A device of wires (typically nitinol) forming a helical cage at the end of a long shaft, which can be opened and closed around a stone.

How it is used

Captures and extracts urinary stones or fragments during rigid or flexible ureteroscopy, under direct or fluoroscopic guidance.
