Payr clamp

Large, heavy intestinal clamp with long jaws and a double joint that multiplies the closing force.

How it is used

Crushing clamp on the segment of bowel or stomach being resected.

Clinical tip

It crushes tissue: use it only on the side being removed and a soft bowel clamp on the side that stays.

How to recognise it

Large, heavy crushing clamp with hinged jaws: stomach or bowel that will be resected.
