Depth gauge

A rod-shaped instrument with a hooked tip and a millimeter scale, inserted into a drilled hole in the bone.

How it is used

Used to measure the depth of a drilled bone hole and determine the correct length of screw to be inserted.

Clinical tip

Measurement accuracy improves with experience, so the reading is often double-checked before picking a long screw.
