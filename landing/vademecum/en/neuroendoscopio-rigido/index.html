Rigid neuroendoscope

A rigid-shaft endoscope (typically about 4 mm in diameter) with a working channel, variable-angle optics and connections to a light source and camera, introduced through a peel-away sheath or trocar.

How it is used

Used in neuroendoscopy to perform endoscopic third ventriculostomy for obstructive hydrocephalus, biopsy or removal of intraventricular lesions, and cyst fenestration.
