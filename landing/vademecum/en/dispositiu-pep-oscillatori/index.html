Oscillating positive expiratory pressure (PEP) device

A handheld device into which the patient blows through an internal mechanism, an oscillating metal ball or a magnet-loaded disc, that generates both an expiratory resistance and an oscillatory vibration transmitted to the airway.

How it is used

Helps mobilize and loosen thick bronchial secretions, as an alternative or complement to manual chest physiotherapy, in patients with conditions that generate large amounts of mucus.

Clinical tip

Unlike the incentive spirometer, which works on inspiration, this device acts on expiration to mobilize secretions, not to prevent atelectasis.
