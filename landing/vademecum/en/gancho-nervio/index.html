Nerve hook

Thin, long-handled instrument with a blunt hook-shaped tip, available in standard or micro sizes for work under the microscope.

How it is used

Palpating, isolating and gently retracting a peripheral or cranial nerve out from among surrounding tissue, without pinching or compressing it.
