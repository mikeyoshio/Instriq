Steam autoclave

A sealed chamber that exposes instruments to saturated steam at high pressure and temperature (typically 121-134°C), with a pre-vacuum stage in porous-load models to remove air from the chamber before steam is admitted.

How it is used

The standard sterilization method in the central sterile supply department (CSSD) for reusable instruments that tolerate heat and moisture, before returning them to clinical use.

Clinical tip

Whether the chamber is vertical or horizontal only affects capacity and loading, not the underlying technology.
