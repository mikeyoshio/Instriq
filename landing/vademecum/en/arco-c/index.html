C-arm (intraoperative fluoroscopy)

A mobile X-ray unit with a C-shaped arm holding the emitter and image detector at opposite ends, providing real-time imaging.

How it is used

Used during surgery to check fracture reduction, implant position (nails, plates, screws) and bone alignment in real time.

Clinical tip

Because it uses ionizing radiation, staff follow time, distance and shielding practices (lead aprons, thyroid shields) to minimize exposure when several exposures are needed in a row.
