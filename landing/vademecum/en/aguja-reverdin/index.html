Reverdin needle

A straight or slightly curved needle with an eye near the tip that opens and closes via a sliding mechanism on the handle, allowing the suture to be threaded or released without withdrawing the needle from the field.

How it is used

Passed by hand, or with minimal instrumentation, through deep tissue or narrow fields where maneuvering a conventionally threaded needle with a needle holder is difficult.

Clinical tip

Unlike the Keith needle (straight, fixed eye) or the swaged needle (no eye), its hinged eye allows the suture to be loaded or released in situ; its current use is limited and it appears mainly in gynecology and general-surgery catalogs for deep stitches.
