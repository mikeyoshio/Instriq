Stainless steel surgical bowl/basin

A round or oval stainless steel container, in various sizes, larger and deeper than an antiseptic cup.

How it is used

Holds saline for field irrigation, immediately-needed swabs and pads, or soaking instruments, depending on its size and how the set-up uses it.
