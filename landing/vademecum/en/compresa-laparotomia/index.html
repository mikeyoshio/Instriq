Laparotomy pad (lap sponge)

A sterile, single-use, large multi-ply cotton gauze pad (roughly 45x45 cm when unfolded) with a radiopaque tape tag sewn into one corner to aid location and retrieval.

How it is used

Used to pack off and protect viscera, retract organs, and absorb large volumes of blood or fluid during open abdominal, pelvic, or other major cavity surgery.

Clinical tip

Always keep the tag visible outside the cavity or secured with a clamp when packing deep; it is a mandatory part of the sponge count, as it is one of the items most often left behind if not tracked.
