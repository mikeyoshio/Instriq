Colibri forceps

A very fine forceps with short jaws and microscopic (0.12 mm) teeth, designed to manipulate the cornea and conjunctiva without tearing them.

How it is used

Used in corneal and cataract surgery to hold the edge of the corneal incision during suturing or wound closure.

Clinical tip

The microscopic (0.12 mm) teeth allow a firm grip on corneal tissue with minimal trauma.
