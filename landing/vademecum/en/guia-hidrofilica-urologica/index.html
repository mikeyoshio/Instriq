Hydrophilic guidewire

Guidewire with a nitinol core and a slippery hydrophilic polymer coating, 0.035" or 0.038", with a straight or angled tip.

How it is used

Negotiates tortuous or stenotic ureteral anatomy to establish and maintain a safe access path for catheters, access sheaths, double-J stents or the ureteroscope itself.
