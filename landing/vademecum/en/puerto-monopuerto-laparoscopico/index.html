Single-incision laparoscopic surgery (SILS) access port

Multi-channel silicone/plastic port placed through one incision, usually umbilical.

How it is used

Passes 3-4 instruments plus the laparoscope while keeping pneumoperitoneum. Used for single-incision cholecystectomy, appendectomy, colectomy and sleeve gastrectomy.
