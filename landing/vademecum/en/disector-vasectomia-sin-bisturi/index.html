No-scalpel vasectomy dissecting forceps

Curved hemostat with one sharpened tip that punctures scrotal skin and spreads the tissue without cutting it.

How it is used

Opens a single scrotal puncture and dissects down to the vas deferens previously fixed with the ring clamp.

Clinical tip

The puncture it leaves is so small it often needs no sutures, just a dressing.
