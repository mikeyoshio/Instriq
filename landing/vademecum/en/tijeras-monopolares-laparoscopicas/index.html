Monopolar laparoscopic scissors

Long-shafted scissors with insulated blades that can be connected to a monopolar electrosurgical generator, allowing the same instrument to coagulate and cut.

How it is used

Used to dissect tissue planes and divide them with simultaneous bleeding control via monopolar energy, in general, colorectal and gynecologic laparoscopic surgery.
