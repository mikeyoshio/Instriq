Neonatal extracorporeal circulation/ECMO system

Compact console with a centrifugal pump, membrane oxygenator and heat exchanger integrated into a single miniaturized circuit.

How it is used

Prolonged extracorporeal cardiopulmonary support in refractory neonatal respiratory or cardiac failure.
