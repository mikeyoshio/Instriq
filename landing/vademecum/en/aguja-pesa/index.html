Percutaneous epididymal sperm aspiration (PESA) needle

Fine needle on a syringe holding a little culture medium.

How it is used

Aspirating sperm from the epididymis in obstructive azoospermia without opening the scrotum.

Clinical tip

The sample goes straight to the fertility lab, as with TESE.
