Nathanson liver retractor

A rigid laparoscopic retractor with an atraumatic curved (hook- or spoon-shaped) blade at the distal end and a hexagonal proximal end that locks into a table-mounted holding arm.

How it is used

Used to elevate and hold back the left hepatic lobe during laparoscopic upper gastrointestinal surgery (such as gastric bypass, sleeve gastrectomy or Nissen fundoplication), since it is held by a fixed table-mounted arm rather than an assistant's hand.

Clinical tip

Sustained compression of the liver can cause transient postoperative liver dysfunction (elevated liver enzymes), so periodically releasing pressure during long procedures is advisable.
