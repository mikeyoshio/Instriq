Rose position (neck hyperextension)

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Family
Supine
Also known as
Rose position, Neck extension

Supine with the neck extended: a roll under the shoulders and the head on a head ring, so the chin points upwards.

Where it is used

Thyroid and parathyroid surgery, tracheostomy, tonsillectomy and anterior neck surgery.

Risks and pressure points

Postoperative neck pain and, in patients with cervical arthritis or disease, risk of spinal cord injury from excessive extension. Pressure on the occiput.

Care and checks

Check the patient's neck mobility beforehand. Extend enough to expose the field, not to the maximum. Keep the head secure so it cannot roll. Protect the eyes.

Related equipment

  • Positioning roll
  • Head ring
  • Arm board
