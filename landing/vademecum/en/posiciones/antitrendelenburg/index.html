Reverse Trendelenburg position

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Family
Supine
Also known as
Reverse Trendelenburg, Head-up, Trendelenburg inverso

Supine variant with the table tilted feet-down: the head is higher than the feet, which rest on a footboard.

Where it is used

Laparoscopic upper abdominal surgery (gallbladder, stomach, hiatus, bariatric surgery), thyroid, and head and neck surgery.

Risks and pressure points

Hypotension from blood pooling in the legs and risk of venous thrombosis. The patient may slide towards the feet. Pressure on the soles if the footboard is not well padded.

Care and checks

Padded footboard with the feet at 90°. Stockings or intermittent pneumatic compression per protocol. Slow tilt changes coordinated with anaesthesia.

Related equipment

  • Table footboard
  • Patient safety strap
  • Operating table (electrohydraulic base unit)
