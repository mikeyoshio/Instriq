Beach chair position

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Family
Sitting
Also known as
Beach chair, Barber chair

Patient sitting with the trunk at about 60-80°, head fixed in a dedicated head rest, hips and knees flexed and the operative shoulder clear of the table edge, arm free or in an articulated arm holder.

Where it is used

Open and arthroscopic shoulder surgery: rotator cuff repair, instability, shoulder replacement.

Risks and pressure points

Hypotension and reduced cerebral perfusion (the brain sits above the level where pressure is measured), air embolism, injury to the great auricular nerve or cervical plexus if the head is turned or tilted, and sacral pressure.

Care and checks

Head neutral and well fixed, no pressure on the eyes or ears. Raise the back in stages. Pillow under the knees and heels free. Opposite arm supported. Stockings or pneumatic compression per protocol.

Related equipment

  • Beach chair positioner
  • Gel positioning pads
  • Patient safety strap
