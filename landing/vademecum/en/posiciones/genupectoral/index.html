Knee-chest position

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Family
Prone
Also known as
Knee-chest position, Andrews frame position, Mohammedan position

Prone variant with the patient kneeling, hips flexed and the chest on the table; for surgery a dedicated frame (Andrews type) with a seat for the buttocks is used.

Where it is used

Lumbar spine surgery (discectomy, laminectomy): flexion opens the interlaminar space and the abdomen hangs free, reducing epidural bleeding.

Risks and pressure points

Pressure on the knees and front of the legs, risk of compartment syndrome and thrombosis from prolonged flexion, and hypotension from blood pooling in the legs.

Care and checks

Knees very well padded and ankles free of pressure. Check distal pulses after positioning. Coordinate every change with anaesthesia.

Related equipment

  • Wilson frame
  • Prone face pillow
  • Gel positioning pads
