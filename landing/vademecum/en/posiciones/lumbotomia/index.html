Kidney (flank) position

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Family
Lateral
Also known as
Kidney position, Flank position, Lateral renal

Lateral position with the operative flank uppermost over the table bridge or a roll, and the table flexed to open the space between the lowest rib and the iliac crest.

Where it is used

Open or laparoscopic surgery of the kidney, proximal ureter and adrenal gland.

Risks and pressure points

As for lateral decubitus, plus hypotension from vena cava compression when the table is flexed, and postoperative back pain.

Care and checks

Flex the table only as much as needed and tell anaesthesia. Return the table to flat before closing the wall so the suture is not under tension. Secure the patient well before flexing.

Related equipment

  • Lateral hip and chest supports
  • Positioning roll
  • Double arm board for lateral position
  • Patient safety strap
  • Operating table (electrohydraulic base unit)
