Fowler (semi-sitting) position

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Family
Sitting
Also known as
Fowler, Semi-Fowler, Semi-sitting

Supine with the table back raised between 30 and 90° (semi-Fowler 30-45°, high Fowler 60-90°), knees slightly flexed and feet on a footboard.

Where it is used

Head and neck, nose and sinus surgery, breast reconstruction, some shoulder surgery, and patients who cannot breathe lying flat.

Risks and pressure points

Orthostatic hypotension, pressure on the sacrum and buttocks, sliding towards the feet and, the more upright, a higher risk of air embolism if the surgical field is above the heart.

Care and checks

Sit the patient up in stages and tell anaesthesia. Pillow under the knees, padded footboard and arms supported so they do not hang. Head well secured.

Related equipment

  • Table footboard
  • Gel positioning pads
  • Patient safety strap
