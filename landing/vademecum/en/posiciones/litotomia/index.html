Lithotomy position

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Family
Supine
Also known as
Lithotomy, Gynaecological position

Supine with the buttocks at the edge of the table and the legs raised and apart in stirrups, hips and knees flexed. Both legs are raised and lowered together.

Where it is used

Perineal, vaginal, anal and endoscopic urological surgery (TURP, ureteroscopy), childbirth and some combined colorectal approaches.

Risks and pressure points

Peroneal nerve injury from the fibular head pressing on the stirrup, and femoral and obturator nerve injury from excessive hip flexion or abduction. Compartment syndrome of the legs in long cases. Hypotension when the legs are lowered. Low back pain.

Care and checks

Raise and lower the legs together and slowly, with two people. Pad the outside of the knee. Watch the patient's fingers when the foot section of the table is raised or lowered. Tell anaesthesia before lowering the legs.

Related equipment

  • Lithotomy stirrups
  • Gel positioning pads
  • Arm board
