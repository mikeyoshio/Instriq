Internal defibrillation paddles

A pair of spoon-shaped electrodes mounted on handles, designed to be applied directly to the myocardium through the open chest.

How it is used

They directly defibrillate or cardiovert the heart during open-heart surgery, using much lower energy (10-20 J) than external defibrillation.
