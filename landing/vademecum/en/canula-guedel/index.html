Guedel cannula

Curved single-use airway placed in the mouth to keep the airway open.

How it is used

Prevents airway obstruction from the tongue falling back in sedated or unconscious patients.

Clinical tip

Size is chosen by measuring from the corner of the mouth to the earlobe.
