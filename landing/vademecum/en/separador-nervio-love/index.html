Love nerve root retractor

Thin hand-held retractor with a blunt, hooked tip, available in straight, angled (45°/90°) or bayonet-shaped versions.

How it is used

Gently holding the nerve root and adjacent soft tissue aside during open lumbar discectomy, to expose the disc space without injuring the nerve.
