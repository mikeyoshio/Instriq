Inflatable penile prosthesis

Implantable device with two intracavernosal cylinders, a scrotal pump and a saline reservoir, joined by silicone tubing.

How it is used

Treats erectile dysfunction: squeezing the pump moves fluid from the reservoir into the cylinders, producing an on-demand erection.

Clinical tip

Coloplast's Titan model includes a lock-out valve that prevents spontaneous inflation if the pump is accidentally squeezed during daily activity.
