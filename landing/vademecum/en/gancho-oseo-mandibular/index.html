Mandibular bone hook

An instrument with a curved, sharp tip mounted on a sturdy handle, designed to anchor onto the inferior border of the mandible and apply traction.

How it is used

Used to manipulate and apply traction to mandibular fragments during fracture reduction, facilitating alignment prior to fixation.

Clinical tip

The tip must be positioned precisely on the bone to avoid slipping and injuring adjacent soft tissue.
