Bailey rib approximator

Two opposing hooks on a ratcheted bar that pull two ribs together as it closes.

How it is used

Bringing the ribs together so pericostal sutures can be tied when closing a thoracotomy.

Clinical tip

Pass the pericostal sutures before applying it and tie them with it closed.
