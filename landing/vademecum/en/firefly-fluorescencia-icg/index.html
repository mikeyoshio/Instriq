Firefly ICG Fluorescence Imaging

Near-infrared imaging mode built into the console endoscope, showing real-time indocyanine green fluorescence.

How it is used

Use: ureter/bile-duct identification, colorectal anastomosis perfusion assessment, sentinel-node mapping, tumor-margin assessment in partial nephrectomy.
