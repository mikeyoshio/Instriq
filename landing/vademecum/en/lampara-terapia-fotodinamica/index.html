Photodynamic therapy lamp

A panel light source with LEDs of a specific wavelength (typically red or blue), designed to activate a photosensitizer previously applied to the skin.

How it is used

Used in photodynamic therapy to treat actinic keratoses, superficial basal cell carcinoma, and acne, activating topical aminolevulinic acid or methyl aminolevulinate.

Clinical tip

The photosensitizer incubation time before light exposure is critical and varies according to the indication and lesion thickness.
