ROSA system (Zimmer Biomet)

Family of robots with a robotic arm on a mobile cart and optical navigation: ROSA Knee and ROSA Hip for arthroplasty, and ROSA ONE for neurosurgery (Brain) and spine (Spine).

How it is used

Knee and hip replacement (planned cuts and ligament balancing), and in neurosurgery electrode placement (SEEG, deep brain stimulation), biopsies and spinal screws.

Clinical tip

Do not cover or knock the navigation markers once registered: if they move, registration must be repeated.
