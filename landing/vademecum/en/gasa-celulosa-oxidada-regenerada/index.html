Oxidized regenerated cellulose hemostatic gauze (Surgicel)

A non-woven fabric of oxidized regenerated cellulose, pale yellow to off-white in color, supplied as sterile sheets or strips that can be trimmed to size; it darkens to almost black on contact with blood.

How it is used

Applied directly onto a bleeding surface (e.g., hepatic or splenic parenchyma, a suture line) as a topical absorbable hemostatic agent that promotes clot formation.

Clinical tip

Use only the minimum amount needed and remove any excess once hemostasis is achieved, since leaving too much in place can promote granuloma formation or act as a nidus for infection; avoid wrapping it constrictively around a vascular anastomosis.
