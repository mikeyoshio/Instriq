Biological sterilization indicator

A vial or strip containing a known population of resistant bacterial spores (typically Geobacillus stearothermophilus for steam, or Bacillus atrophaeus for ethylene oxide), incubated after the cycle to check whether any spore survived.

How it is used

The definitive confirmation that a sterilization cycle was lethal to the most resistant microorganisms, not just that the physical process conditions were reached.

Clinical tip

Unlike chemical indicators, the result is not immediate: it must be incubated for hours (or minutes, with rapid enzymatic-readout systems) before a result is available.
