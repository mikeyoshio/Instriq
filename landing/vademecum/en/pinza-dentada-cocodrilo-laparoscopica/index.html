Crocodile-tooth laparoscopic grasper

Laparoscopic grasper with long, toothed jaws ("crocodile" design), for a firm grip on slippery tissue -- a descriptive name for the tooth pattern, not a specific brand.

How it is used

Used to retract and manipulate tough or slippery tissue that needs a firmer grip than an atraumatic grasper provides.
