Neonatal phototherapy unit

A panel or overhead unit of lights, usually blue LEDs at a specific wavelength, placed above the newborn's crib or incubator.

How it is used

Treats neonatal hyperbilirubinemia by breaking down bilirubin in the skin into soluble isomers that are excreted without needing hepatic conjugation.

Clinical tip

The newborn's eyes are always protected with an opaque eye mask during treatment.
