Mikulicz pack (Mikulicz drain)

A large sterile gauze sac or bag (classically fashioned from a single piece of gauze with a central drawstring) packed with smaller sterile gauze rolls or sponges, left with a tape or suture tag exiting the wound.

How it is used

Packed loosely into a large, contaminated, or infected cavity (e.g., after drainage of an abscess or in an open-abdomen situation) to promote hemostasis, absorb exudate, and keep the cavity open; the inner filling is removed in stages over subsequent days, with the outer bag withdrawn last to minimize trauma to granulating tissue.

Clinical tip

Document it as a single counted unit with a clearly visible external tag, and plan a staged removal (typically starting at 48-72 hours) to avoid both premature cavity collapse and retained gauze.
