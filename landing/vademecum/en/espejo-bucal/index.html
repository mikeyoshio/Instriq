Mouth mirror

Flat or magnifying round mirror, about 2 cm across, on a slim handle. Part of the basic dental examination set together with the explorer and cotton forceps.

How it is used

Indirect view of tooth surfaces that cannot be seen directly, retraction of tongue and cheek, and reflecting light onto the working area.

Clinical tip

It fogs with the patient's breath: warm it slightly or apply anti-fog before starting.
