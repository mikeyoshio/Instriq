Arndt endobronchial blocker

A thin catheter with an inflatable balloon tip and a guide loop, introduced alongside a conventional endotracheal tube under bronchoscopic guidance.

How it is used

Used as an alternative to the double-lumen tube to isolate one lung (or a lobe) and achieve one-lung ventilation in thoracic surgery.
