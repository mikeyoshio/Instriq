Incentive spirometer

A simple device with one or more balls or a piston inside a transparent chamber, which rises in proportion to the inspiratory flow the patient generates while inhaling through a tube.

How it is used

Used postoperatively to encourage deep, sustained breaths that prevent atelectasis and respiratory complications, giving immediate visual feedback that motivates the patient's effort.

Clinical tip

It must be stressed that the effort should be a slow, sustained inspiration, not a quick gasp, so the balls rise steadily and the alveoli open properly.
