Rigid sterilization container

A reusable aluminum or polymer box with a gasketed lid and a bacterial filter or valve system that lets steam or gas in but blocks microorganisms afterward.

How it is used

An alternative to textile wrap or pouches for packaging instrument sets before sterilization and keeping them sterile in storage, commonly used for large or heavy trays.

Clinical tip

The filter or gasket must be inspected and replaced on the manufacturer's schedule, since a worn seal breaks sterility with no visible sign.
