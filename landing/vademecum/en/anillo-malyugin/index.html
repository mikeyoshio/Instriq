Malyugin ring

A single-use polypropylene device shaped as an eight-point scalloped ring, designed to be introduced folded through a small incision.

How it is used

Used in small-pupil cataract surgery to mechanically expand and maintain pupil dilation throughout the procedure.

Clinical tip

It is removed at the end of surgery with a specific deployment/removal instrument, once the intraocular lens has been implanted.
