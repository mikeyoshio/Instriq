Bispectral index (BIS) monitor

A monitor that processes the patient's frontal EEG and reduces it to a dimensionless index (0-100) correlated with hypnotic depth.

How it is used

Monitoring of anesthetic depth to prevent intraoperative awareness, especially under neuromuscular relaxation.

Clinical tip

It is the most widely used objective tool precisely in deeply sedated or relaxed patients, where subjective sedation scales are unreliable.
