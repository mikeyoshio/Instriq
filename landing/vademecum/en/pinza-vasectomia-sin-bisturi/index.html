No-scalpel vasectomy ring clamp

Narrow-ring clamp (3.5 mm) that grasps the vas deferens through intact skin without piercing it.

How it is used

Used in no-scalpel vasectomy to immobilize and deliver the vas deferens before the skin puncture.

Clinical tip

Designed by Li Shunqiang in 1974; by avoiding a scalpel skin incision, it lowers hematoma and infection rates compared with the conventional technique.
