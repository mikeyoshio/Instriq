Angioplasty balloon catheter

A long, flexible catheter with an inflatable balloon of specific diameter and length mounted at the tip, connected to a pressure-controlled inflation device.

How it is used

Used in percutaneous transluminal angioplasty to dilate arterial or venous stenoses, and to deploy stents in the same procedure.

Clinical tip

Inflation pressure must be monitored with a dedicated gauge (indeflator) to avoid exceeding the balloon's rated burst pressure.
