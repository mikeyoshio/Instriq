Minnesota retractor

A long, flat, slightly curved retractor with a blunt edge, designed to retract the cheek and tongue and facilitate access to the oral cavity.

How it is used

Used in oral and maxillofacial surgery, especially third molar extractions and intraoral orthognathic surgery, to expose the surgical field.

Clinical tip

Its rounded edge minimizes trauma to the oral mucosa during prolonged retraction.
