Resectoscope loop electrode

Single-use wire loop (monopolar or bipolar) mounted on two guide rods that lock into the resectoscope's working element; cuts and coagulates tissue chip by chip.

How it is used

The actual cutting consumable in transurethral resection of the prostate (TURP) or bladder (TURBT), interchangeable on the resectoscope's reusable body.
