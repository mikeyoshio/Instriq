Cryotherapy device / cryoprobe

Device that releases a controlled micro-spray of nitrous oxide or liquid nitrogen through a probe or spray tip.

How it is used

Freeze destruction of benign skin lesions (warts, actinic keratoses, seborrheic keratoses).
