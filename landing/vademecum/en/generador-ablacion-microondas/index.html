Microwave ablation generator

Generator with a cooled needle antenna that creates a zone of heat necrosis around its tip.

How it is used

Destroying liver and other tumours by open, laparoscopic or percutaneous approach.

Clinical tip

Prime the cooling circuit before activation; radiofrequency ablation, an alternative, also needs a return electrode.
