Arterial introducer sheath

A short flexible plastic tube with a hemostatic valve at the proximal end and an internal dilator, designed to facilitate repeated percutaneous access to a vessel.

How it is used

Used in endovascular procedures to establish and maintain stable vascular access, allowing exchange of catheters, guidewires, and devices.

Clinical tip

The French size should be chosen based on the largest device that will need to pass through during the planned procedure.
