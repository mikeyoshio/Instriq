Handheld cortical stimulator (Ojemann-type)

A portable, battery-powered device with a bipolar probe whose two tips sit a few millimeters apart, delivering an adjustable constant current directly onto the cortical surface.

How it is used

Used to intraoperatively map eloquent cortical areas (motor, sensory or language) during awake or anesthetized craniotomy, maximizing tumor resection while preserving function.
