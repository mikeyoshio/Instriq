Ureteral access sheath (UAS)

A hydrophilic-coated, kink-resistant plastic tube placed over a guidewire into the ureter before the flexible ureteroscope is passed through it.

How it is used

Facilitates repeated passage of the flexible ureteroscope into the kidney during retrograde intrarenal surgery (RIRS), protects the scope and reduces intrarenal pressure.

Clinical tip

Lowers intrarenal pressure and allows the scope to be withdrawn and reinserted without losing access, useful when multiple fragments must be removed.
