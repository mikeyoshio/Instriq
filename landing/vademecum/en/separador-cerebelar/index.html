Cerebellar retractor

Self-retaining retractor with a flexible arm connecting a brain blade or spatula to a fixation system, built to hold steady retraction in a deep, narrow space.

How it is used

Exposing the posterior fossa in cerebellopontine angle surgery or other posterior fossa approaches, where sustained manual retraction would be impractical.
