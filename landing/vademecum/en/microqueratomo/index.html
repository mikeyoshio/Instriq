Microkeratome

A motorized instrument with an oscillating steel blade mounted on a carriage that advances across a suction ring fixed to the eye, cutting corneal tissue to a preset depth.

How it is used

Cuts the hinged corneal flap in LASIK refractive surgery, applying suction to the eye to raise and flatten the cornea before the blade advances; a mechanical alternative to femtosecond-laser flap creation.

Clinical tip

Used after the corneal marker (which has already marked orientation): the microkeratome cuts the flap, and the marks allow it to be correctly repositioned at the end.
