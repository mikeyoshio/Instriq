Bite block (rubber mouth prop)

Silicone or rubber block in several sizes, often with a retrieval cord, placed between the molars to keep the mouth open.

How it is used

Keeping the mouth open during oral surgery under sedation or general anaesthesia, on the side opposite the surgical site.

Clinical tip

Always keep the cord outside the mouth or tied: if it slips, it can obstruct the airway. Include it in the final count.
