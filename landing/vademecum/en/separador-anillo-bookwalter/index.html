Self-retaining ring (Bookwalter-type) retractor

Table-fixed retractor system: a ring or frame with interchangeable blades locked onto flexible posts, holding the wound open without needing an assistant's hands.

How it is used

Deep abdominal or pelvic exposure in radical cystectomy, open retropubic radical prostatectomy, or open nephrectomy.
