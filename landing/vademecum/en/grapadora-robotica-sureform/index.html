SureForm Robotic Stapler

A fully wristed EndoWrist instrument with a motorized staple-and-cut mechanism (SmartFire) that senses tissue compression during firing, controlled from the robotic console.

How it is used

Creates staple lines and transects tissue during robotic anastomoses and resections (colorectal, pulmonary, bariatric, gynecologic), with articulation that eases approach to tissue in difficult positions.
