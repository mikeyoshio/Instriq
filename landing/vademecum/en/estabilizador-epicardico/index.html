Epicardial stabilizer (Octopus-type)

Vacuum- or compression-based mechanical arm that locally immobilizes a small area of beating epicardium.

How it is used

Allows a coronary anastomosis to be performed on the beating heart without cardiopulmonary bypass (off-pump CABG, OPCAB).
