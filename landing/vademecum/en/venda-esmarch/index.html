Esmarch bandage

Wide elastic rubber or latex bandage wrapped firmly from the distal end of the limb upwards.

How it is used

Exsanguinating the limb just before the tourniquet is inflated.

Clinical tip

Not used with infection, tumour or suspected thrombosis in the limb, as it could spread. Check for latex allergy.
