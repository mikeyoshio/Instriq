Artificial urinary sphincter

Implantable device with a periurethral occlusive cuff, a pressure-regulating balloon, and a scrotal control pump the patient activates manually.

How it is used

Restores continence in severe male stress urinary incontinence, typically after prostatectomy.

Clinical tip

The patient deflates the cuff by squeezing the scrotal pump before voiding; it automatically re-inflates after a few minutes.
