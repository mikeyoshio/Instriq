Cardiotocograph (electronic fetal monitor)

An electronic device with two transducers strapped onto the maternal abdomen with elastic belts: one uses ultrasound to detect the fetal heart rate, and the other (tocodynamometer) senses abdominal wall tension during contractions.

How it is used

It continuously or intermittently records the fetal heart rate and uterine contraction pattern during labor (or during a prepartum non-stress test) to detect early signs of fetal compromise.

Clinical tip

A tracing with reduced variability and decelerations does not always indicate true fetal compromise: it should always be interpreted in the full clinical context rather than in isolation.
