CO2 insufflator

An electronic device connected to a carbon dioxide source that regulates and delivers gas through tubing to the trocar, controlling insufflation pressure and flow.

How it is used

Used to establish and maintain pneumoperitoneum throughout laparoscopic surgery, holding a preset intra-abdominal pressure and automatically replacing gas lost through leaks.
