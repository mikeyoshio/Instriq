Plug-and-patch hernia device

A pre-shaped monofilament polypropylene plug with pleated petals that fills the inguinal or femoral ring, attached to a flat reinforcing patch; a tension-free repair technique distinct from flat Lichtenstein mesh.

How it is used

Primary open inguinal or femoral hernia repair, typically under local or regional anesthesia through a short incision.

Clinical tip

Should not be used in contaminated or infected wounds, as the mesh can cause fistula or extrusion; it should also not directly contact bowel.
