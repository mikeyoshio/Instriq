Targeted temperature management system

A device that, using water-circulating blankets or intravascular catheters, keeps the patient's body temperature at a precise target value for hours or days, with automatic feedback from a temperature probe.

How it is used

Used after a resuscitated cardiac arrest to prevent fever and, in some protocols, induce mild-to-moderate hypothermia, aiming to reduce secondary neurological injury.

Clinical tip

Unlike an already-catalogued passive warming blanket, this system actively controls both warming and cooling within a very narrow margin.
