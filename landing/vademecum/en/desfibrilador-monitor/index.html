External defibrillator-monitor

A device that delivers a biphasic electrical shock (typically up to 200-360 J) via external paddles or adhesive pads to reverse lethal arrhythmias; it integrates ECG, SpO2, capnography and non-invasive blood pressure monitoring, plus non-invasive pacing.

How it is used

Mandatory resuscitation equipment in every OR, to treat cardiac arrest or malignant intraoperative arrhythmias; distinct from the already-catalogued internal defibrillation paddles, which are applied directly to the open-chest myocardium.

Clinical tip

Typical technical specifications require a charge time under 10 seconds up to 200 J, with both adult and pediatric pad sets.
