Potts-Smith scissors

A scissor with long, thin, angled blades (typically 45-60°), designed for precise cutting in deep, narrow spaces.

How it is used

Used to open and extend arteriotomies and for fine vascular dissection in adult cardiovascular surgery; the angled blade keeps the cutting line clearly visible.
