Pinard horn (fetal stethoscope)

A trumpet- or truncated-cone-shaped instrument made of wood or aluminum, with no electronic parts, rested with one end on the maternal abdomen and the other against the examiner's ear.

How it is used

Basic fetal auscultation with no need for electricity or conductive gel, still used as a low-cost or backup method when no Doppler is available.
