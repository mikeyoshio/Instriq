Laparoscopic fan retractor

A laparoscopic retractor whose distal tip deploys several fan-shaped blades when a proximal dial is turned (or, in the paddle variant, an inflatable cushion), providing a wide, soft contact surface.

How it is used

Used to steadily retract the liver, stomach or bowel loops during upper abdominal laparoscopic surgery, spreading pressure over a wide surface to reduce tissue trauma.

Clinical tip

Watch the blades closely while closing the fan, since an organ trapped between them during retraction or removal can be injured.
