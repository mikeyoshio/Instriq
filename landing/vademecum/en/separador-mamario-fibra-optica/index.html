Lighted (fiber-optic) breast retractor

A surgical retractor with a smooth, curved blade connected via a fiber-optic cable to a light source, which directly illuminates the depth of the breast pocket while retracting tissue.

How it is used

Used in breast augmentation and reduction (and in mastectomy/reconstruction) to retract tissue while simultaneously illuminating the depth of the implant pocket, improving hemostasis and dissection precision through small incisions.

Clinical tip

Always use the fiber-optic cable diameter recommended by the manufacturer (e.g., 3.0 mm): a cable wider than the connector generates heat at the junction, and burns from contact with skin or drapes have been documented.
