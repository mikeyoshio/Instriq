Symani Surgical System (robotic supermicrosurgery)

Robotic platform with NanoWrist instruments replicating hand motion at sub-millimeter scale.

How it is used

Use: lymphaticovenous anastomosis and immediate lymphatic reconstruction after axillary dissection, on 0.3-0.8 mm vessels.
