Sterilization pouch/reel

A single-use wrap made of medical-grade paper and clear film (as a self-seal pouch or a reel cut to size), or non-woven fabric sheeting for larger wraps.

How it is used

Packages instruments before sterilization and keeps them sterile until opened on the field, allowing the contents to be seen without breaking the barrier.

Clinical tip

Pouches and reels suit individual items or small sets; non-woven wrap is used for large trays and sets, closed by folding rather than sealing.
