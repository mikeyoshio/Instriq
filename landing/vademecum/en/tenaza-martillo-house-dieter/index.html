House-Dieter malleus nipper

A ring-handled micro-instrument with a thin shaft and delicate jaws at the tip: one sliding jaw cuts as it closes against the other, fixed jaw, which ends in a small sharpened anvil or hook.

How it is used

Used in ossicular chain surgery (tympanoplasty, ossiculoplasty, stapedectomy) to transect the malleus (e.g., its neck or head) under microscopic view, as a step before removing it or placing a prosthesis.

Clinical tip

Part of the otology instrument sets associated with William House, a pioneer of modern neurotology; it has its own dedicated US regulatory product code ("Nipper, Malleus"), confirming its status as a standardized instrument rather than a generic micro-scissor.
