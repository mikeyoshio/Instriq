Rigid sigmoidoscope

Long rigid tube with an obturator, light source and an insufflation bulb, marked in centimetres.

How it is used

Examining the rectum, taking biopsies and measuring a lesion's distance from the anal verge.

Clinical tip

Read the distance on the markings at the anal verge and record it: it decides the surgical technique.
