Surgical needle curvature classification

Suture needles are classified by their curvature in fractions of a circle — straight, 1/4 (90°), 3/8 (135°), 1/2 (180°), and 5/8 — which determines the hand's turning radius when passing the needle through tissue.

How it is used

The instrumentista selects curvature based on how confined and deep the surgical field is: tighter curves (1/2, 5/8) for narrow/deep spaces, open curves (1/4, 3/8) for superficial or wide-access fields.

Clinical tip

3/8 and 1/2 circle curvatures are the most commonly used in general surgery, while 5/8 is reserved for confined spaces such as urology or the pelvis.
