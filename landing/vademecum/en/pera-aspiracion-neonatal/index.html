Neonatal bulb suction aspirator

Single-piece soft rubber bulb with no parts or external suction source: it is emptied by squeezing it, and suction is created as it springs back into shape.

How it is used

Gentle suctioning of a newborn's mouth and nose right after birth to clear secretions, when stronger wall suction is not needed.
