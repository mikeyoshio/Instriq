Catheter valve (plug)

A small lever-tap valve that connects to the catheter outlet in place of a continuous drainage bag, opened manually by nursing staff or the patient to empty the bladder.

How it is used

Allows ambulation without a visible bag and, in selected patients, bladder training (clamping and opening every few hours to maintain bladder tone) before catheter removal; its routine use for this last purpose is debated, since a 2023 systematic review found higher infection risk and delayed first voiding for short-term catheters compared with continuous free drainage.
