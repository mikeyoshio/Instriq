Suture anchor

Small implant (metal, PEEK, or bioabsorbable) preloaded with suture strand(s), fixed into bone.

How it is used

Reattaches a tendon or ligament to bone; the single most-used implant in shoulder arthroscopy (rotator cuff repair).
