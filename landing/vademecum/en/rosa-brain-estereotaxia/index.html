ROSA Brain Stereotactic Neurosurgery Robot

High-precision robotic stereotactic positioning arm guiding intracranial electrodes/instruments.

How it is used

Use: SEEG electrode implantation for refractory epilepsy, stereotactic brain biopsy, DBS lead placement.
