Peripheral nerve stimulator (train-of-four monitor)

A device that delivers a series of electrical stimuli (typically a train-of-four) to a peripheral nerve via surface electrodes and records or observes the resulting muscle response.

How it is used

Used to monitor the degree of neuromuscular blockade produced by muscle relaxants during general anesthesia, and to confirm adequate recovery before extubation.

Clinical tip

A train-of-four ratio (TOFR) of 0.9 or greater indicates full recovery of muscle strength and is considered the safe threshold for extubation.
