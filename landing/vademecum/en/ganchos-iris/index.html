Iris hooks (iris retractors)

Thin hooks (usually 4, in a square or diamond configuration) that engage the pupillary margin through paracentesis incisions to retract and maintain dilation of a small or rigid pupil.

How it is used

Phacoemulsification with a small pupil (pseudoexfoliation, intraoperative floppy iris syndrome, synechiae), as an alternative to Malyugin-type expansion rings.

Clinical tip

Dilation with hooks should be limited to what is necessary (~5 mm), as they can damage the pupillary margin, especially in rigid pupils.
