Laparoscopic needle holder

A long-shafted instrument (around 33 cm) with short, sturdy jaws, a pistol-grip or axial handle and a locking mechanism, designed to firmly hold the suture needle through a trocar.

How it is used

Used for intracorporeal suturing and knot-tying in laparoscopic surgery (such as vaginal cuff closure, fundoplication or bowel suturing), often with two needle holders working together to tie the knot.
