Laryngeal mask airway (LMA)

Supraglottic airway device with an inflatable cuff that seals around the laryngeal inlet.

How it is used

Alternative to intubation for short procedures that do not require full muscle relaxation.

Clinical tip

Does not protect against gastric content aspiration — contraindicated when aspiration risk is high.
