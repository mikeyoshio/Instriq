Fogarty biliary balloon catheter

A dedicated balloon catheter (5-6 Fr, 23 or 40 cm), regulated as its own device category, distinct from the already-catalogued arterial embolectomy Fogarty balloon catheter: different balloon diameter, fill volume, and clinical indication.

How it is used

Passed via choledochotomy or the cystic duct beyond a retained stone, inflated, then withdrawn to sweep the stone out of the common bile duct during open bile duct exploration.
