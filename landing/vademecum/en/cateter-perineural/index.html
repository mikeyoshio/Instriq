Continuous peripheral nerve (perineural) catheter

A flexible catheter inserted alongside the target nerve (after localization with a needle) and connected to an infusion pump for continuous local anesthetic delivery.

How it is used

Prolonged postoperative analgesia after orthopedic/limb surgery, placed in the OR by the anesthesia team itself.

Clinical tip

The use of continuous perineural catheters improves at-home pain control and reduces opioid side effects.
