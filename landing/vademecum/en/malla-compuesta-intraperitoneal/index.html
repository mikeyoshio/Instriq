Composite intraperitoneal mesh

Two-sided mesh: a polypropylene or polyester face that integrates into the wall and an anti-adhesion face towards the viscera.

How it is used

Repair of ventral and incisional hernias where the mesh touches the bowel.

Clinical tip

Check which face goes towards the viscera before insertion; do not open it until the surgeon confirms the size.
