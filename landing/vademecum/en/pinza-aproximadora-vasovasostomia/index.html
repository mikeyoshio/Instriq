Vas deferens microspike approximator clamp

Double- or triple-armed clamp with fine steel microspikes that grip only the adventitia of the two vas deferens ends.

How it is used

Holds both vas deferens ends (or the epididymis) aligned and motionless during microsurgical suturing in vasovasostomy and vasoepididymostomy.

Clinical tip

The microspikes engage only the adventitia without compressing the mucosa, preventing slippage without needing strong clamping force.
