Intra-aortic balloon pump

A catheter with a cylindrical polyurethane balloon at its tip, inflated with helium in synchrony with the cardiac cycle and connected to an external console.

How it is used

It is placed in the descending aorta to increase coronary perfusion and reduce left ventricular afterload, providing mechanical circulatory support in cardiogenic shock or high-risk cardiac surgery.
