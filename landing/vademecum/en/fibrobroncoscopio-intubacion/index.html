Flexible fiberoptic bronchoscope (for intubation)

A thin flexible endoscope with a fiberoptic or digital sensor tip and a working channel, allowing direct visualization of the airway while the endotracheal tube is advanced over it.

How it is used

Used for fiberoptic intubation, awake or under sedation, in patients with anticipated or known difficult airway, guiding the tube into the trachea under direct vision.

Clinical tip

Awake fiberoptic intubation is considered the gold-standard technique for securing the airway in cases of known difficult airway.
