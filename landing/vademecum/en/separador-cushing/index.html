Cushing (decompression) retractor

Hand-held retractor with a curved, fenestrated blade at the tip, designed to hold back and protect brain tissue from the craniectomy edge during decompression.

How it is used

Manual brain retraction during decompressive craniectomy or other intracranial approaches where soft tissue needs to be held back from the bone edge.
