Medical gas cylinder (oxygen)

A high-pressure-resistant metal cylinder, painted with a standardized color code according to the gas it contains, fitted with a pressure-reducing valve and a gauge.

How it is used

Supplies oxygen, or more rarely nowadays nitrous oxide, in a portable way when a wall medical gas outlet is not available, such as during patient transport.

Clinical tip

Nitrous oxide cylinders share the same general design but carry a different color code and must never be confused with oxygen ones.
