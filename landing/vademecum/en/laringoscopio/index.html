Laryngoscope (handle + blade)

Handle with a light and interchangeable blade (curved Macintosh or straight Miller) to visualize the glottis.

How it is used

Orotracheal intubation: displaces the tongue and epiglottis to view the vocal cords before inserting the tube.

Clinical tip

The Macintosh blade is placed in the vallecula (in front of the epiglottis); the Miller lifts the epiglottis directly.
