Bakri postpartum balloon

Silicone balloon catheter (maximum capacity ~800cc, typically filled to ~500cc) placed transvaginally into the uterine cavity to tamponade bleeding.

How it is used

Second- or third-line treatment for postpartum hemorrhage from uterine atony, once uterotonics and bimanual compression have failed, before resorting to surgery or hysterectomy.

Clinical tip

A 132-patient series reported a 95.4% success rate, avoiding hysterectomy in most cases.
