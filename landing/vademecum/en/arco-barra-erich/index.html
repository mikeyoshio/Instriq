Erich arch bar

A malleable, perforated metal bar fitted with hooks, wired to the teeth to permit intermaxillary fixation.

How it is used

Used in mandibular and maxillary fractures to establish intermaxillary fixation, guiding dental occlusion during bone healing.

Clinical tip

It must be carefully adapted to each patient's dental curve before wiring to avoid unwanted occlusal forces.
