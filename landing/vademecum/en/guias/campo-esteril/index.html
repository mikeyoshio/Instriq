Sterile field and back table setup

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Asepsis
Also known as
Sterile field, Back table setup, Aseptic technique, Mesa de instrumental

The sterile field is the area around the patient and tables where only sterile items and scrubbed staff belong. Setting it up and keeping it sterile is the whole team's job.

Before opening

  • Check the pack: intact, dry, in date and with the chemical indicator changed.
  • Open as close as possible to the start of surgery.
  • Clean tables at working height; no draughts directed at the field.

Setting up

The scrub nurse arranges instruments in order of use, in a consistent layout any colleague can follow. Check each instrument's integrity and function and the internal indicator in each container. The Mayo stand holds only what will be used next.

Aseptic principles

  • If in doubt about sterility, consider it contaminated.
  • Sterile touches only sterile; scrubbed staff pass back to back.
  • Pack edges and drape edges are not sterile.
  • A wet drape is no longer a barrier: cover it with a dry one.
  • Never leave an opened field unattended.
