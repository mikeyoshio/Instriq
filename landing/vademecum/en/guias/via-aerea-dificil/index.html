Difficult airway trolley

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Safety
Also known as
Difficult airway trolley, Carro de vía aérea difícil, Difficult airway

When ventilation or intubation fails, every second counts. The difficult airway trolley holds the kit in an order the whole team knows, and the nurse should find each item without searching.

What is in it

  • Videolaryngoscope with blades in several sizes; laryngoscope with alternative blades.
  • Tracheal tubes in several sizes, stylets and bougie.
  • Supraglottic devices (second-generation laryngeal masks) in several sizes.
  • Flexible intubation scope.
  • Front-of-neck access kit (cricothyroidotomy): scalpel, bougie and small cuffed tube.

During the crisis

Call for help aloud, have suction ready and the capnograph connected: the CO2 trace confirms intubation. The anaesthetist follows the local algorithm; the nurse passes what is asked for and records times.

Checking

Check the trolley daily or per protocol, seal it and restock it straight after use.

Related equipment

  • Video laryngoscope
  • Intubating stylet (bougie/stylet)
  • Second-generation supraglottic airway (i-gel)
  • Flexible fiberoptic bronchoscope (for intubation)
  • Capnograph (end-tidal CO2 monitor)
