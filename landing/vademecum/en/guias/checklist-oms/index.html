WHO surgical safety checklist

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Safety
Also known as
WHO surgical safety checklist, Listado de verificación quirúrgica, Time out, Pausa quirúrgica

A list of checks at three points in the operation that reduces wrong patient, site and procedure errors and avoidable complications. It works when the whole team stops and takes part aloud.

1. Before induction of anaesthesia

  • Patient identity, site, procedure and consent confirmed.
  • Site marked, if applicable.
  • Anaesthesia equipment check done and pulse oximeter working.
  • Known allergies.
  • Difficult airway or aspiration risk.
  • Risk of major blood loss and blood available.

2. Before skin incision (time out)

  • Everyone introduces themselves by name and role.
  • Patient, site and procedure confirmed aloud.
  • Surgeon: critical steps, duration and expected blood loss.
  • Anaesthesia: patient-specific concerns.
  • Nursing: sterility confirmed (indicators) and equipment issues.
  • Antibiotic prophylaxis given within the right time.
  • Essential imaging displayed.

3. Before the patient leaves theatre

  • Name of the procedure performed.
  • Swab, needle and instrument counts correct.
  • Specimens labelled, reading the label aloud including the patient's name.
  • Equipment problems to be addressed.
  • Key concerns for the patient's recovery.

Instriq's own text, based on general recommendations (WHO and perioperative nursing guidelines). Educational reference: it does not replace your hospital's protocol.
