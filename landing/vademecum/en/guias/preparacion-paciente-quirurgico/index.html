Preparing the surgical patient on the ward

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Techniques
Also known as
Preoperative preparation, Preparación preoperatoria

What happens on the ward before transfer decides whether surgery starts on time and without avoidable risk. Each hospital has its own protocol and checklist.

The day before and the morning

  • Fasting as anaesthesia instructs (solids and clear fluids have different times).
  • Shower with soap, or antiseptic if protocol says so, and clean clothes.
  • If hair must be removed, use clippers on the day, never a razor.
  • Usual medication: continue or stop only as the anaesthetist indicated.

Before transfer

  • ID wristband, signed consents and notes with test results.
  • Allergies recorded and visible.
  • Surgical site marked by the surgeon if required.
  • No jewellery, piercings, make-up, nail polish or artificial nails; dentures, glasses and hearing aids removed or recorded.
  • Empty bladder, IV access if protocol requires, and observations recorded.

Handover

At handover to theatre, confirm aloud identity, procedure, side, fasting, allergies and anything pending. Say whatever is not in the record.

Instriq's own text, based on general recommendations (WHO and perioperative nursing guidelines). Educational reference: it does not replace your hospital's protocol.
