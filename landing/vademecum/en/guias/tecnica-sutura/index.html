Suturing technique

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Techniques
Also known as
Suturing technique, Técnica de sutura, Puntos de sutura

Basic principles for skin suturing and for loading and passing sutures from the table. Material choice (absorbable or not, size, needle) depends on the tissue and the surgeon.

Loading and passing

  • Grip the needle between its middle and rear thirds, never at the point or the swage.
  • Pass the needle holder with the needle point towards the surgeon and the thread kept under control.
  • Every needle that leaves the table must come back and be counted.

Stitch types

  • Simple interrupted: the most common; each stitch separate.
  • Continuous: quicker, but if it breaks the whole line opens.
  • Vertical or horizontal mattress: everts the edges in thick or tense skin.
  • Subcuticular: better scar, no stitches on the surface.

Principles

Enter perpendicular to the skin, at the same distance and depth on each side, edges slightly everted and not tight: tissue will swell. Square, secure knots. Removal is roughly 5-7 days on the face to 10-14 on the trunk and limbs, as the surgeon decides.

Related equipment

  • Mayo-Hegar needle holder
  • Adson forceps, toothed
  • Mayo scissors
  • Needle counter
