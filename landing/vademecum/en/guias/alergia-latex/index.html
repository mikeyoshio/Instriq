Latex-allergic patient

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Safety
Also known as
Latex allergy, Latex-free theatre, Quirófano libre de látex

Latex allergy can cause anaphylaxis during surgery. Risk is higher in people with many previous operations (spina bifida, urological malformations), healthcare workers and those allergic to banana, avocado or kiwi.

Preparing the theatre

  • First on the list if that is local practice, in a clean theatre.
  • "Latex-free" sign on the door and bed.
  • Remove or cover anything containing latex: gloves, catheters, drains, tourniquets, BP cuffs, rubber tubing, vial stoppers.

During surgery

Whole team in latex-free gloves. Unexplained hypotension, bronchospasm or sudden flushing must be reported to the anaesthetist: it may be anaphylaxis.

Checking

Check each label for the latex symbol and, if in doubt, the hospital's latex-free product list.
