Patient with obesity

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Patient care
Also known as
Bariatric patient, Obesity, Paciente bariátrico

Severe obesity changes transfer, positioning, airway management and pressure-injury risk. All equipment must be rated for the patient's weight.

Before arrival

  • Check the weight limit of the table, side extensions and trolley.
  • Transfer mattress or hoist and enough staff for the transfer.
  • Correctly sized BP cuff, long instruments and long trocars for laparoscopy.

Airway

Intubation may be difficult and desaturation rapid. Pillows or a wedge to raise the trunk and head (ramped position) and the difficult airway trolley nearby.

Positioning

Strap and side supports secured before tilting the table. Pad all pressure points and check skin folds for moisture and pressure injury.

Related equipment

  • Operating table (electrohydraulic base unit)
  • Vacuum positioning mattress
  • Lateral hip and chest supports
  • Gel positioning pads
  • Video laryngoscope
