Recovery position

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Emergencies
Also known as
Recovery position, Posición lateral de seguridad, PLS

For an unconscious person who is breathing normally: keeps the airway open and lets vomit or secretions drain. Based on the ERC first aid recommendations (2025).

How to do it

  • Nearest arm at a right angle, palm up.
  • Other arm across the chest, back of the hand against the cheek.
  • Bend the far knee and roll the person towards you by pulling on the knee.
  • Tilt the head slightly back to keep the airway open.

Afterwards

Keep checking that breathing is normal. If it stops, turn the person on their back and start CPR. If they stay a long time, switch sides periodically to protect the lower arm from pressure.

Instriq's own text, based on general recommendations (WHO and perioperative nursing guidelines). Educational reference: it does not replace your hospital's protocol.
