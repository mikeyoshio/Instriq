Surgical site marking and laterality

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Safety
Also known as
Site marking, Wrong-site surgery, Universal Protocol, Lateralidad

Wrong-site, wrong-side or wrong-patient surgery is rare but serious and preventable. Three steps prevent it: verify, mark and pause before incision.

Verify

Check name, procedure and side against the patient (if able to speak), consent, notes and imaging at every handover: ward, reception and theatre.

Mark

  • Done by the operating surgeon or their team, before sedation and with the patient awake if possible.
  • With a permanent marker that survives prepping, at or near the incision, visible after draping.
  • Where marking is impossible (mucosa, teeth, neonates), the hospital needs an alternative system.

Pause

Right before incision the whole team stops and confirms aloud the patient, procedure and side, and that the mark is visible in the field. This is the second phase of the WHO checklist.

Instriq's own text, based on general recommendations (WHO and perioperative nursing guidelines). Educational reference: it does not replace your hospital's protocol.
