Paediatric patient in theatre

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Patient care
Also known as
Paediatric patient, Niño en quirófano

Children are not small adults: they lose heat quickly, tolerate fasting poorly and any dose or volume error matters far more. All equipment is chosen by weight and age.

Temperature

Warm theatre before arrival, radiant warmer or forced-air blanket, head covered in the youngest and warmed fluids. Expose only what is needed.

Equipment

  • Today's weight recorded and visible to everyone.
  • Airway equipment in the right size plus one size up and down.
  • Paediatric or neonatal return electrode and reduced-size instruments.
  • IV fluids through volume-controlled sets to avoid overload.

Family

Where allowed, a parent may stay until induction. Explain what will happen in simple words and let the child keep a comfort object.

Related equipment

  • Radiant infant warmer
  • Forced-air warming system
  • Reduced-caliber pediatric laparoscopic trocar
