Choking in infants

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Emergencies
Also known as
Choking infant, Atragantamiento en el lactante

Under one year, no abdominal thrusts: back blows are combined with chest thrusts. Based on the ERC recommendations (2025).

If coughing effectively

Let the infant cough and watch closely.

If the cough is ineffective

  • Face down along the forearm, head lower than the body, supporting the jaw: up to 5 back blows.
  • Turn face up and give up to 5 chest thrusts, at the CPR position but sharper and slower.
  • Look in the mouth and remove the object only if visible; never do a blind finger sweep.

If the infant becomes unconscious

Call 112 and start paediatric CPR with 5 rescue breaths.
