Preparing the operating microscope

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Equipment
Also known as
Operating microscope setup, Preparación del microscopio

The microscope is used in neurosurgery, ENT, ophthalmology, plastic surgery and microsurgery. It must be balanced, focused and draped before the surgeon needs it.

Before draping

  • Position it on the surgeon's side, brakes on and out of the way.
  • Turn on the light and check intensity; set eyepieces (dioptres and interpupillary distance) with the surgeon.
  • Balance the arm for the configuration to be used, with the assistant's eyepiece if needed.

Drape

Two people drape it: the scrub nurse unrolls the drape over the head and arms without touching anything unsterile, and the circulating nurse helps from outside. Fit the lens cover without bubbles.

Safety

The light is very intense: turn it down when not aimed at the field so it does not burn tissue or drapes. Lock the brakes afterwards and return it to the transport position.
