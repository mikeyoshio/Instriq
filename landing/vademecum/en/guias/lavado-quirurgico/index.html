Surgical hand antisepsis

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Asepsis
Also known as
Surgical hand antisepsis, Surgical scrub, Lavado de manos quirúrgico

Removes dirt and reduces hand and forearm flora as far as possible before putting on a sterile gown and gloves. It can be done with an alcohol-based rub or with antiseptic soap and water.

Before you start

  • Short nails, no polish or artificial nails.
  • No rings, watch or bracelets.
  • Cap covering all hair and a well-fitted mask before scrubbing.
  • First scrub of the day: clean under the nails with a nail cleaner.

Alcohol-based rub

Clean, dry hands. Apply the product to hands and forearms up to the elbows in several applications, following the manufacturer's time and technique (usually 2 to 5 minutes in total). Let it dry completely before gloving.

Antiseptic soap

Wet hands and forearms, apply the soap and scrub area by area (fingers, palms, backs, wrists, forearms) for the manufacturer's time. Rinse from fingertips to elbow, keeping the hands above the elbows so water does not run back. Dry with a sterile towel.

Common mistakes

  • Touching the tap, mask or clothing after scrubbing: start again.
  • Gloving while the alcohol is still wet.
  • Using a brush on the skin: it irritates and does not improve the result.

Related equipment

  • Surgical scrub brush
  • Sterile surgical gown
