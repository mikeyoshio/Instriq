Choking in adults

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Emergencies
Also known as
Choking adult, Obstrucción de la vía aérea por cuerpo extraño, OVACE

Airway obstruction by a foreign body. First decide whether the person can still cough effectively. Based on the ERC first aid recommendations (2025).

If coughing effectively

Encourage them to keep coughing and watch closely, nothing more.

If the cough is ineffective

  • Up to 5 back blows between the shoulder blades, with the person leaning forward.
  • If it does not clear, up to 5 abdominal thrusts.
  • Alternate until it clears or the person becomes unconscious.

If they become unconscious

Lay them down, call 112 and start CPR. Anyone who received abdominal thrusts must be seen by a doctor.

Instriq's own text, based on general recommendations (WHO and perioperative nursing guidelines). Educational reference: it does not replace your hospital's protocol.
