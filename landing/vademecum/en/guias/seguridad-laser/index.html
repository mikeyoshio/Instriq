Laser safety

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Safety
Also known as
Laser safety, Seguridad láser

Surgical lasers can burn the retina, skin and drapes and ignite oxygen-rich gases. Each laser has its own wavelength and needs matching protection.

Before activation

  • Warning sign on the doors and windows covered if protocol requires.
  • Glasses rated for that laser's wavelength for everyone in the room, and eye protection for the patient.
  • Laser on standby until the surgeon is aimed at tissue.

Fire

In airway surgery, use a laser-resistant tracheal tube with the cuff filled with saline, oxygen as low as the anaesthetist allows and moist swabs around the field. Keep saline at hand.

Instruments

Use matt or blackened instruments so the beam does not reflect. Laser plume is evacuated like any surgical smoke.

Related equipment

  • Laser safety goggles
  • Laser-resistant endotracheal tube (Laser-Flex-type)
  • CO2 laser for laryngeal microsurgery
