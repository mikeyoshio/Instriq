PPE: putting on and taking off

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Asepsis
Also known as
PPE donning and doffing, Colocación y retirada del EPI

Personal protective equipment only protects if put on in the right order and, above all, taken off without self-contamination. Removal is the riskiest moment.

Putting on

  • Hand hygiene.
  • Gown, tied securely.
  • Mask or respirator; for respirators, do a seal check.
  • Goggles or face shield.
  • Gloves, over the gown cuffs.

Taking off

  • Gloves (or gloves and gown together, rolling them inside out).
  • Hand hygiene.
  • Gown, touching only the inside.
  • Goggles or shield, by the arms or strap.
  • Mask or respirator, by the straps, outside the contaminated area if protocol says so.
  • Hand hygiene.

Good practice

Follow your hospital's sequence, which may vary with the risk. For high-risk situations, take it off with a colleague watching. Never touch your face with gloves on.

Related equipment

  • FFP2/FFP3 (N95-equivalent) respirator for aerosol-generating procedures
  • Sterile surgical gown
