Surgical count

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Safety
Also known as
Surgical count, Recuento de gasas, Retained surgical item

Counting swabs, sponges, needles and instruments prevents items being left inside the patient. It is done aloud by the scrub and circulating nurses and recorded.

When to count

  • Before starting (baseline count).
  • When items are added.
  • Before closing a cavity within a cavity (e.g. the uterus before the abdomen).
  • Before closing the fascia and before closing the skin.
  • When the scrub or circulating nurse changes.

How to count

Both nurses look and count aloud together, separating each item. Only radiopaque swabs and sponges in the field. Never cut swabs or sponges. Used needles go in the needle counter. Nothing counted leaves the theatre until the end.

If the count is wrong

Tell the surgeon immediately and before closing. Search the field, wound, floor and bins. If it is not found, X-ray per protocol. Record it and report it as an incident even if it turns up.

Related equipment

  • Radiopaque surgical gauze swab (4x4 sponge)
  • Laparotomy pad (lap sponge)
  • Gauze pledget (torunda ball)
  • Needle counter
