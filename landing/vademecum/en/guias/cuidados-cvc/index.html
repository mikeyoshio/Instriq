Central venous catheter care

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Techniques
Also known as
Central line care, CVC care, Cuidados del catéter venoso central

Central line infections are serious and largely preventable. Care is aseptic and always done the same way.

Insertion site

  • Hand hygiene and gloves; alcoholic chlorhexidine, allowed to dry.
  • Transparent dressing, changed per protocol or sooner if soiled, wet or loose.
  • Watch for redness, discharge, pain or unexplained fever.

Connections

Scrub the hub with antiseptic and let it dry before every access. Handle it as little as possible, clamp unused lumens and change sets according to what runs through them (lipids and blood need shorter intervals).

Removal

Patient lying flat, head down if possible; ask them to hold their breath as it comes out and cover at once with an occlusive dressing to prevent air embolism. Check the catheter is intact.

Related equipment

  • Central venous catheter
  • Transparent film dressing
