Setting up arthroscopy

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Equipment
Also known as
Arthroscopy setup, Montaje de artroscopia

Arthroscopy works inside a fluid-filled joint: besides camera and light it needs a pump or gravity system to distend it, the shaver and often radiofrequency.

Before starting

  • Tower opposite the joint with the monitor in the surgeon's line of sight.
  • Plenty of irrigation bags close by: they run out fast.
  • Pump pressure as the surgeon sets; shaver and radiofrequency pedals at their foot.

During surgery

Change bags before they run dry so no air enters. Watch the outflow volume and the limb: fluid leaking into tissues makes it swell and must be reported.

The floor

A lot of fluid is used: fluid-collection drapes, floor suction or absorbent mats prevent slips.

Related equipment

  • Arthroscope
  • Arthroscopic shaver
  • Fluid management / distension pump system
  • Endoscopy camera control unit (CCU) and light source
  • Pneumatic surgical tourniquet
