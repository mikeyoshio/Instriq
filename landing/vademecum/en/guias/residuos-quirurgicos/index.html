Waste and instruments after surgery

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Equipment
Also known as
Theatre waste, Waste segregation, Gestión de residuos

Theatres produce a lot of waste. Sorting it properly protects those who handle it, meets regulations and cuts cost and environmental impact.

Sorting

  • Sharps into the rigid container, never into a bag.
  • Items heavily soiled with blood or fluids into clinical waste per local rules.
  • Clean packaging and plastics into general waste or recycling.
  • Cytotoxics, chemicals and anatomical parts have their own pathway.

Instruments

At the end the scrub nurse removes blades and needles, opens hinged instruments, separates delicate items and keeps instruments moist (foam or damp towel) so blood does not dry before reaching sterile services. Check no piece of the set is missing.

In the theatre

Dispose of suctioned fluid per protocol (often with a gelling agent), not down the sink. Clean surfaces before the next patient.

Related equipment

  • Sharps container
  • Needle counter
