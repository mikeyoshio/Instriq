Arterial line: set-up and monitoring

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Techniques
Also known as
Arterial line, Línea arterial, Invasive blood pressure

An arterial line gives beat-to-beat blood pressure and allows blood gas sampling. The doctor inserts it; nursing prepares, calibrates and monitors the system.

Preparing the system

  • Flush bag per protocol in a pressure bag inflated to about 300 mmHg.
  • Prime the whole circuit and transducer with no bubbles.
  • Have the puncture, securing and dressing kit ready.

Calibrating

  • Transducer level with the phlebostatic axis: fourth intercostal space, mid-axillary line.
  • Open to air and zero; repeat if the patient or table position changes.
  • Fast-flush test: the trace should return within one or two oscillations; if damped or ringing, check bubbles, kinks and connections.

Safety

Label it as arterial: never inject anything into it. Watch the colour, warmth and sensation of the hand or foot. A disconnection can bleed fast: keep connections tight and visible.

Related equipment

  • Arterial catheter (arterial line)
