Adult basic life support

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Emergencies
Also known as
Adult basic life support, RCP básica, SVB

When someone is unresponsive and not breathing normally, every minute without compressions reduces survival. Review summary based on the European Resuscitation Council (ERC 2025 guidelines).

Recognise and call

  • Make sure the scene is safe.
  • Unresponsive and not breathing normally (gasping is not normal breathing): cardiac arrest.
  • Shout for help, call 112 or the arrest team and ask for a defibrillator.

Compressions

  • Centre of the chest, arms straight, on a firm surface.
  • Depth 5-6 cm at 100-120 per minute, allowing full recoil.
  • 30 compressions to 2 breaths if trained and able; otherwise continuous compressions.
  • Swap every 2 minutes to keep quality up.

Defibrillator

Switch it on and follow its prompts as soon as it arrives. No one touches the patient during analysis or shock, and compressions restart immediately.

Related equipment

  • External defibrillator-monitor
  • Self-inflating resuscitation bag
