Setting up the laparoscopy tower

Educational reference: always follow your hospital's protocol and the anaesthesia and surgical team's instructions.

Area
Equipment
Also known as
Laparoscopy tower setup, Montaje de torre de laparoscopia

The tower holds the camera, light source, insufflator and monitor. Setting it up and checking it before the patient arrives avoids delays and insufflation errors.

Before the patient arrives

  • Tower opposite the surgeon with the monitor facing them; a second monitor for the assistant if needed.
  • Switch everything on and check there is a picture.
  • CO2 cylinder open with enough gas and a spare nearby.
  • Maximum insufflation pressure as set by the surgeon or protocol.

In the field

Pass the camera cable, light cable and insufflation tubing from field to tower, with a filter if supplied. White-balance on a white swab and focus before entering the abdomen. Warm the scope or use anti-fog.

Safety

  • The light cable tip burns: never leave it on the drapes without a scope attached; keep the source on standby when not in use.
  • Watch pressure and flow on the insufflator and report alarms.
  • Have the open conversion set ready.

Related equipment

  • Endoscopy camera control unit (CCU) and light source
  • CO2 insufflator
  • Laparoscope
  • Veress needle
  • Trocar
  • Surgical smoke evacuation system
