Blood glucose meter (glucometer)

A portable device that measures glucose concentration in a drop of capillary blood using an electrochemical test strip, with a reading in seconds.

How it is used

Used for spot glycemic control in critically ill, perioperative or diabetic patients, to adjust insulin dosing or detect hypoglycemia or hyperglycemia.

Clinical tip

Some combined models also measure capillary blood ketones with a different strip, useful for detecting ketoacidosis.
