Implant surgery motor (physiodispenser)

Control unit with foot pedal, peristaltic irrigation pump and micromotor that sets the speed and maximum torque of the handpiece.

How it is used

Drilling the implant bed and inserting the implant with torque control, in implant and oral surgery.

Clinical tip

Before starting, check the programmed handpiece ratio (e.g. 20:1) and that the irrigation line is sterile and primed: drilling without cooling overheats and kills bone.
