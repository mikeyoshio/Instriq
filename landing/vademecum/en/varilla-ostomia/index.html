Loop ostomy rod/bridge

A short (~90 mm), single-use, rigid-but-smooth rod, or a plastic bridge, passed under the exteriorized bowel loop to mechanically support it at skin level.

How it is used

Placed through the mesentery of a loop colostomy/ileostomy when it is fashioned, to prevent the loop retracting back into the abdomen before it matures; typically removed around postoperative day 5.

Clinical tip

A Brazilian series of 209 patients using a T-shaped metal bridge prevented stoma retraction in all cases, with skin discomfort as the main drawback; an Australian cohort, by contrast, found retraction was rare (0.78%) even without a rod.
