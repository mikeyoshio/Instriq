Pulse oximeter

A noninvasive device, typically clipped onto a finger, that uses two wavelengths of light to estimate arterial hemoglobin oxygen saturation and pulse rate.

How it is used

Used to continuously monitor a patient's oxygenation during anesthesia, sedation, and in critical care, enabling early detection of hypoxemia.

Clinical tip

The introduction of pulse oximetry is considered one of the advances that has most improved perioperative safety since its widespread adoption.
