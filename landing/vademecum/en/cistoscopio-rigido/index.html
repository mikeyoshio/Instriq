Rigid cystoscope

A rigid endoscope consisting of a metal sheath with an obturator and a lens telescope, passed through the urethra into the bladder.

How it is used

Used to visually examine the urethra and bladder (haematuria, tumours, stones, strictures) and, through its working channel, to take biopsies, fulgurate lesions or place catheters/stents.
