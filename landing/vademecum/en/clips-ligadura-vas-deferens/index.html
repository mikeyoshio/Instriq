Titanium ligating clips for vas deferens occlusion

Titanium hemoclips applied to each cut end of the vas deferens as an alternative/adjunct to suture ligation, preventing the folded vas from slipping free of the ligature.

How it is used

Occlusion of the vas deferens during conventional or no-scalpel vasectomy, instead of or alongside chromic suture.
