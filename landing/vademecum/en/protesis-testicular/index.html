Testicular prosthesis

Solid silicone elastomer implant filled with saline, shaped and sized like a natural testicle.

How it is used

Placed in the scrotum for cosmetic restoration after orchiectomy or in cases of testicular agenesis.

Clinical tip

A single-use sizing guide with four sizes (7 to 20 cc) is available to select the implant before the definitive incision.
