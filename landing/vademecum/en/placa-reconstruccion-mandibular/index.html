Mandibular reconstruction (load-bearing) plate

Titanium plate much thicker and more rigid than a standard osteosynthesis miniplate, preformed or patient-specific CAD/CAM-made, designed to bridge and load-bear across a mandibular continuity defect.

How it is used

After segmental mandibulectomy for tumor or a severely comminuted fracture, alone or with bone graft, to preserve mandibular continuity.

Clinical tip

Plate exposure risk is associated with fewer residual teeth, prior radiotherapy, and longer defects.
