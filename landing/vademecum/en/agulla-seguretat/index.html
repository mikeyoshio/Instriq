Safety-engineered needle

A hypodermic needle with a built-in mechanism, a retractable sheath or a hinged shield, that automatically covers the tip right after it is withdrawn from the patient.

How it is used

Reduces the risk of accidental needlestick injury and bloodborne pathogen transmission to healthcare staff, a regulatory requirement in many countries for all sharp material.

Clinical tip

The mechanism must be activated immediately after use, before letting go of the needle, since it offers no protection if exposure time is prolonged.
