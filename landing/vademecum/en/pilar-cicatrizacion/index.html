Cover screw and healing abutment

Titanium parts that screw into the implant: the cover screw sits flush under the gum; the healing abutment protrudes through it.

How it is used

Protecting the implant's internal connection during osseointegration and shaping the gum for the future crown.

Clinical tip

They are tiny: hand them over seated on the screwdriver, with a throat gauze if the patient is not intubated, to avoid swallowing or aspiration.
