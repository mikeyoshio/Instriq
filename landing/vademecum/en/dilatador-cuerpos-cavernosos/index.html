Corpora cavernosa dilators

Set of progressively larger metal rods, straight (Hegar) or curved-tip (Brooks), that gradually dilate the corpora cavernosa.

How it is used

Used before penile prosthesis implantation to create the space needed for the cylinders inside each corpus cavernosum.

Clinical tip

In the penoscrotal approach, the flat, straight shape of Hegar dilators can hinder distal dilation toward the glans, which is why curved-tip Brooks dilators are often preferred for that step.
