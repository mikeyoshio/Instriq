Direct-vision optical trocar

Transparent, non-cutting conical-tip trocar with the laparoscope seated inside during insertion.

How it is used

Visualizes each abdominal-wall layer during entry. Used for first entry in patients with prior surgery/adhesions, reducing inadvertent vascular/bowel injury.
