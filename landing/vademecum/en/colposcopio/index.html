Colposcope

Binocular optical instrument with magnification (typically 6-40x) and its own light source, used to examine the cervix, vagina and vulva after applying acetic acid or Lugol's iodine to highlight abnormal epithelium.

How it is used

Standard follow-up to an abnormal cervical cytology or HPV test, to locate the area for a directed biopsy.
