neuromate (stereotactic robot)

Renishaw stereotactic robot, fixed to the table and the patient's head holder, that aligns a guide with the trajectory planned on MRI and CT.

How it is used

Functional and stereotactic neurosurgery: deep brain stimulation electrodes, SEEG for epilepsy, biopsies and neuroendoscopy.
