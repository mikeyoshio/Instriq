Electrosurgical loop electrode (LEEP/LLETZ)

Single-use thin wire loop electrode connected to an electrosurgical generator, used to excise the cervical transformation zone in a single pass.

How it is used

Diagnostic and therapeutic excision of cervical intraepithelial neoplasia (CIN) after an abnormal cytology or colposcopy, as an outpatient procedure.
