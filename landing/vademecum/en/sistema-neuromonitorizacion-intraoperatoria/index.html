Intraoperative neurophysiological monitoring (IONM) system

Equipment combining amplifiers, stimulation/recording software and surface or needle electrodes attached to the patient, operated by a neurophysiologist from within the operating room.

How it is used

Records real-time somatosensory evoked potentials (SSEP), motor evoked potentials (MEP) and other signals to flag the risk of neurological injury during spine surgery, brain or spinal cord tumor resection, and cerebrovascular surgery.
