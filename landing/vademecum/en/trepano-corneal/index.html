Corneal trephine

A cylindrical instrument with a circular cutting edge, of calibrated diameter, designed to make a precise circular cut through the full thickness of the cornea.

How it is used

Used in penetrating keratoplasty to cut the recipient's and donor's corneal buttons with exactly matching diameters.

Clinical tip

Diameter precision between the donor and recipient buttons is critical to minimize postoperative graft astigmatism.
