Bowman probe

A thin, fine, malleable metal probe, double-ended with different calibers, designed to explore and dilate the lacrimal drainage system.

How it is used

Used in lacrimal probing to diagnose and treat nasolacrimal duct obstruction, especially in children with congenital epiphora.

Clinical tip

It should be advanced gently following the natural anatomy of the duct to avoid creating a false passage.
