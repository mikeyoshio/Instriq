Rigid thoracoscope

A rigid endoscope with optics and a light source, connectable to a video camera, introduced through a port in the chest wall.

How it is used

Provides visualization of the pleural cavity for examination, biopsy, and video-assisted thoracoscopic surgery (VATS).
