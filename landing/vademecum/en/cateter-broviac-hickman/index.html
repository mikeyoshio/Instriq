Broviac/Hickman tunneled catheter

Tunneled silicone central venous catheter with a subcutaneous Dacron cuff that induces fibrosis to anchor it and reduce infection/migration risk.

How it is used

Long-term venous access in neonates and infants (even under 1,000g) for parenteral nutrition, chemotherapy, or hemodialysis when an implantable port is not used.

Clinical tip

In infants, placement via peripheral venous cutdown is often preferred when direct central access is difficult.
