Low-flow oxygen therapy system (nasal cannula and face mask)

Devices that deliver supplemental oxygen at a fixed flow (nasal cannula) or via a face mask with or without a reservoir bag and one-way valves (non-rebreather mask), providing a variable inspired oxygen fraction depending on flow and the patient's breathing pattern.

How it is used

Used in any patient needing supplemental oxygen, from low requirements (nasal cannula) up to fractions close to 100% with a non-rebreather reservoir mask.

Clinical tip

Unlike humidified high-flow systems, these devices do not guarantee a fixed FiO2 because it depends on the room air the patient entrains while breathing in.
