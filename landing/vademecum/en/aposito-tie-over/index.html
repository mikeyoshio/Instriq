Tie-over bolster dressing

A gauze or foam bolster placed over a skin graft and secured by suture ends (placed radially around the graft margin) tied directly over it, delivering sustained downward pressure.

How it is used

Immobilizes a skin graft (e.g. after burn or oncologic resection coverage) against the wound bed to prevent shear, and helps reduce hematoma/seroma underneath.

Clinical tip

A review concludes that tie-over dressings and quilting sutures are clinically equivalent in outcome, so the choice is largely surgeon preference.
