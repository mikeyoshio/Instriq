Gillies hook

A fine single sharp-pointed hook mounted on a lightweight pencil-style handle, designed to manipulate soft tissue with minimal trauma.

How it is used

Used in plastic and reconstructive surgery to manipulate and retract the skin edge during suturing, avoiding the crush injury forceps would cause.

Clinical tip

Its pencil-grip handling allows very fine control, essential in facial surgery and cutaneous microsurgery.
