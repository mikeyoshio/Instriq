Endotracheal tube (ETT)

Flexible single-use tube with an inflatable cuff, in different sizes (measured in mm of internal diameter).

How it is used

Secures the airway and allows mechanical ventilation during general anesthesia.

Clinical tip

Typical size in adult women: 7-7.5 mm; in adult men: 7.5-8 mm.
