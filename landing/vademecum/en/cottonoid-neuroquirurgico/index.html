Neurosurgical cottonoid (patty)

A small, sterile, thin, flat pad of compressed rayon or cotton felt, rectangular or strip-shaped, with an incorporated radiopaque thread or band; supplied in several graduated sizes.

How it is used

Placed over exposed brain, spinal cord, or nerve tissue in neurosurgery and microsurgery to protect and gently retract it, keep it moist, and absorb small amounts of blood or irrigation fluid without adhering to it.

Clinical tip

Moisten it before placement and keep strict visual tracking and counting at all times, since its small size allows it to migrate within deep, narrow surgical corridors, making it a specifically recognized retained-foreign-body risk in neurosurgery.
