Swan-Ganz catheter

A long, flexible catheter with an inflatable balloon tip that, once inserted into a central vein, is flow-directed by the bloodstream into the pulmonary artery.

How it is used

It allows real-time monitoring of cardiac output, pulmonary pressures, and other hemodynamic parameters during high-risk cardiac surgery and in critically ill patients.
