External ventricular drain (EVD) catheter

A soft, flexible plastic catheter with multiple side holes near its tip, inserted through a burr hole into the lateral ventricle and connected to a closed external sterile collection system.

How it is used

Allows temporary drainage of cerebrospinal fluid and monitoring of intracranial pressure in acute hydrocephalus, intraventricular hemorrhage or traumatic brain injury.
