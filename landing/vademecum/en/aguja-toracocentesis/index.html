Thoracentesis needle/catheter

A set consisting of a puncture needle and a flexible catheter advanced over the needle into the pleural space, typically paired with a three-way stopcock.

How it is used

Used to puncture the pleural space and withdraw fluid or air for diagnostic purposes (pleural fluid analysis) or therapeutic relief of an effusion or pneumothorax.
