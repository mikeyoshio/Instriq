Knee cutting guide / jig

Metal block/template fixed to the femur or tibia that guides the oscillating saw per the planned resection angles.

How it is used

Produces the femoral (distal/anterior/posterior/chamfer) and tibial cuts with correct alignment, in total knee arthroplasty.
