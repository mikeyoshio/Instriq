Interbody fusion cage and inserter (PLIF/TLIF/ALIF)

Titanium or PEEK spacer implanted into the evacuated intervertebral disc space to restore disc height and promote bony fusion, placed with a dedicated handled inserter instrument.

How it is used

Lumbar or cervical interbody fusion after discectomy, via a posterior (PLIF), transforaminal (TLIF), or anterior (ALIF) approach; complements the already-catalogued pedicle screw system.

Clinical tip

The TLIF approach only requires removing the facet and lamina on one side (unlike the bilateral PLIF approach), reducing nerve-root retraction.
