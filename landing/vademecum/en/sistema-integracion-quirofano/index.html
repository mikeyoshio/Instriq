Integrated operating room system

Ceiling-mounted displays, a touch console and a video router that bring together cameras, endoscopy, imaging and recording.

How it is used

Routing any video source to any screen, recording or streaming the procedure and, in some systems, controlling lights and devices.

Clinical tip

Check before starting that video reaches the surgeon's screen; recording requires the consent the hospital specifies.
